# PREMISE BANK v2 — extension 43–78, plus the production material

Companion to `PREMISE_BANK.md` (1–42). Same voice, same rules. Premise 42 is the
end of the original; these continue the numbering so `[[HUMOR_BLUEPRINT]]` and
`[[SCRIPT_TEMPLATE]]` can cite one continuous bank.

**The three rules every premise obeys:**
1. The joke is the world refusing to explain itself. Never punchline-first.
2. Someone is completely calm about something impossible. The calm IS the joke.
3. No premise requires dialogue to land. It must read from a silent frame.
