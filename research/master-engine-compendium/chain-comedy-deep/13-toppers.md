## Core Concept

A "topper" is a joke that tops the previous joke — it's bigger, weirder, or more absurd than the last one. In vaudeville and stand-up, toppers were used to escalate a bit: each joke topped the previous one until the bit reached its peak.

## The Structure

1. **Joke 1**: The setup (normal situation)
2. **Joke 2**: The punchline (something absurd happens)
3. **Topper 1**: The absurd thing gets weirder
4. **Topper 2**: The weirder thing gets impossible
5. **Topper 3**: The impossible thing breaks reality

## The WuBu Topper

In a 10-second bumper, the toppers are compressed:
- 0-3s: Normal hallway
- 3-5s: Hallway is breathing (punchline)
- 5-7s: Breathing gets louder (topper 1)
- 7-9s: Walls start moving (topper 2)
- 9-10s: The Old UNC sighs (topper 3 / button)
