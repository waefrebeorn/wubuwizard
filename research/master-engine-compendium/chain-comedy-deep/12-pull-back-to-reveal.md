## Core Concept

"Pull back to reveal" is a camera technique that changes the audience's understanding of what they're seeing. The camera pulls back (or zooms out) to reveal that the scene is not what it appeared to be. The audience's mental model is rewritten. That rewrite is the laugh.

## Examples

- A character appears to be standing in a normal room. Camera pulls back: they're on a stage. The "room" is a set.
- A character appears to be having a normal conversation. Camera pulls back: they're talking to a chicken. The "person" is a chicken.
- A character appears to be tall. Camera pulls back: they're standing on a box. The "height" is a prop.

## The WuBu Pull Back

The height opposition uses pull-back-to-reveal:
- The Old UNC appears to be the same height as everyone else.
- Camera pulls back: he's towering over short people. The "normal" was a lie.
- The audience's mental model is rewritten. That's the laugh.
