# 🔭 ~ WUBU ~ LENS LIBRARY — "What-If" Angles
> *Fuel for [[HUMOR_BLUEPRINT]] Step 2.* Take a [[PREMISE_BANK]] premise, apply a
> lens, get a scene. Each lens = a recurring WuBu angle.

## WORLD-RULE LENSES (from [[UNIVERSE]] world rules)
- **L1 Height Opposition** — reframe any scene through tall/short power dynamics.
- **L2 Silence-as-Language** — the unsaid is the joke; characters "read" silence.
- **L3 Order Ritual** — ordinary action performed as sacred ceremony.
- **L4 Dimension-Storage** — a storage unit opens to something that isn't a unit.
- **L5 Found Room** — a real place, then the generated impossible added to it.

## PERSONA LENSES
- **L6 Old UNC Deadpan** — the world goes weird; he doesn't.
- **L7 Colonel Codec** — any topic as urgent→info→personal→non-sequitur→[STATIC].
- **L8 Seymour Ponderable** — any topic as erudite absurd insight + clipart + quote.
- **L9 Laura Counter-Voice** — the straight(ish) foil to the UNC's stillness.

## FORMAT-COLLISION LENSES (from [[GENRE_OVERLAP]])
- **L10 Game-Show Framing** — anything as a competition with a teal-orb contestant.
- **L11 Horror Defused** — dread setting, benign by the UNC's calm.
- **L12 Anthology Self-Contain** — one bit, one joke, no continuation required.

## BOOTLEG LENSES (from [[BOOTLEG]])
- **L13 Onion Headline** — deadpan fake-news structure, WuBu name.
- **L14 Eric Andre Chaos** — controlled cable-access breakdown.
- **L15 Robot Chicken Swap** — replace one element of a known thing with the absurd.

## EXAMPLE (premise + lens → scene)
- Premise 23 ("game shows are rigged") + L10 (Game-Show Framing) + L6 (Deadpan)
  → Old UNC hosts "Who Wants to Understand the Old UNC"; orb wins by silence;
  scoreboard ∞:1. (See [[CONTINUITY_WEB]] callback #S-5.)
