# NODE 9: The Onion / ClickHole — Satirical News, Deadpan Irony, Memeing the News Format

## Core concept
The Onion = the satirical-news-as-invasion model: a fake news format that INVADES the news format and memeing it from within. The master engine's news/commentary mode should work the same way: invade the news format, deadpan irony, no jokes visible.
