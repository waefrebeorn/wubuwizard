# NETWORK IDENTITY — WuBuTV

**Named by the human: WuBuTV.** 2026-09-26. This is the network's name. It is
not a proposal and it is not open for a vote.

## The ruling
**Structure B — A Foundation.** @WuBuOriginals (the MEGA, the found room, the
diegetic day-stamps) is the flagship. Every other channel is understood as part
of the same production company, and the network openly admits it: *the room was
real, the crowd was generated, and this is the paperwork.*

The conceit, straight from premise 32 and N-3: **WuBuTV is a foundation that
documents itself.** It is not a channel, not a platform — it is the *found*
thing the other channels are made from, and the paperwork is the content. The
News channel explains the network to itself; Labs and MediaSchool are the
network admitting what it is.

## The load-bearing rule
WuBuTV never claims to be a foundation and never explains itself, same as every
other piece of this canon. **If an episode ever has a character acknowledge the
network by name, that is a bug** — in the same class as UNC acknowledging the
other channels, which is the load-bearing rule of the whole continuity web.

**Exception — equipment only, never a person.** A monitor, slate, lower-third,
log, or ident may display `WuBuTV`. A character may not say it and no dialogue
may reference it.
