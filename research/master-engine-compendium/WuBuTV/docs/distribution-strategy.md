## The 2026 TikTok Algorithm (What Actually Matters)

### Signal Hierarchy (Strongest to Weakest)
1. **Watch time / Completion rate** (STRONGEST — 70%+ required to go viral)
2. **Rewatch rate** (15-20% = strong signal — one viewer watching 3x beats 3 viewers once)
3. **Shares** (high weight)
4. **Comments, follows from video** (strong)
5. **Likes, hashtag engagement** (moderate)
6. **Device type, language, location** (weakest)

### The 2026 Changes
- **70% completion rate required** (up from 50% in 2024)
- **Follower-first distribution**: Your followers are the test audience. If they don't engage, your video dies.
- **Rewatch rate multiplies reach**: People replaying = massive algorithmic boost
- **Longer videos winning**: 60-180s outperforming 15s (but bumpers are 10s — fine for ads, not main content)
- **Original audio gets priority**: Trending sounds work, but original audio gets a boost
- **TikTok is a search engine**: 64% of Gen Z searches on TikTok like Google
- **Engagement velocity**: Fast engagement in first hour = wider reach

### What Kills Your Reach
- Completion rate below 70%
- Weak engagement from your own followers in first hour
- Posting when your audience is asleep
- Inconsistent posting
- Generic content (algorithm detects and buries it)
- Unoriginal content (watermarked, simple GIFs)
- Clickbait hooks that don't deliver

## Hook Writing (First 3 Seconds = 80% of Viral Potential)

### Proven Hook Types
1. **Provocative question**: "Why did nobody tell me this sooner?"
2. **Unbelievable claim**: "This one trick completely changed how I clean my house."
3. **Start mid-action**: Open with a crash, splash, or chaos, then rewind.
4. **Bold on-screen text**: "You're not going to believe what happens next"
5. **Visual pattern interrupt**: Something that breaks the scroll pattern

### Bad vs Good Hooks
- ❌ "Hey guys, today I want to talk about..."
- ✅ "This TikTok mistake is killing your views."
- ❌ "Welcome back to my channel..."
- ✅ "I found something impossible."

## Viral Psychology (Why People Share)

### High-Arousal Emotions (Get Shared)
- Joy, surprise, awe, humor (positive)
- Anger, anxiety, shock (negative)
- ALL of these trigger sharing

### Low-Arousal Emotions (Get Likes, Not Shares)
- Sadness, contentment, calm
- These feel good but don't compel sharing

### Social Currency (Why People Share)
- **Looking smart**: Sharing insightful/clever content
- **Being helpful**: Sharing useful tips
- **Staying relevant**: Sharing trending content
- **Expressing identity**: Sharing content that captures their beliefs

### The Story Gap
A well-structured narrative creates a "story gap" — pose a problem or question at the start, the viewer feels a psychological pull to close that loop by watching to the end.

## Platform-Specific Strategy

### TikTok
- **Format**: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920
- **Length**: 60-180s for main content, 10s for bumpers/ads
- **Completion rate**: 70%+ required
- **Audio**: Original audio gets priority boost
- **Posting**: 3-5x/week minimum, when audience is online
- **SEO**: Keyword-rich captions, searchable on-screen text

### Instagram Reels
- **Format**: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920
- **Length**: 15-90s optimal
- **Recompresses hardest**: Feed CRF 18 quality
- **Hashtags**: Still matter (5-10 relevant)
- **Cover image**: Custom cover performs better

### YouTube Shorts
- **Format**: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920
- **Length**: Under 60s (the 59-second rule)
- **Color tags**: Must be explicit BT.709 or looks washed out
- **Title**: Short, keyword-rich
- **#Shorts**: Tag helps discovery

### X (Twitter)
- **Format**: 16:9 or 1:1 (horizontal/square preferred)
- **Length**: Under 2 minutes optimal
- **Hook**: Must be in first sentence (no "Thread:" preamble)
- **Video native**: Upload directly, don't link to YouTube

## Measurement Framework

### Key Metrics Per Video
1. **Completion rate** (target: 70%+)
2. **Rewatch rate** (target: 15%+)
3. **Share rate** (target: 5%+)
4. **Views in first hour** (engagement velocity)
5. **Follower conversion** (follows from video)

### Kill Criteria
If a video gets <50% completion rate after 100 views → the hook failed. Don't post similar content until you fix the hook.

### A/B Testing
For each concept, generate 2 versions:
- Version A: Question hook
- Version B: Statement hook
- Measure which gets higher completion rate
- Winner becomes the template for future content
