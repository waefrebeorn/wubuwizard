## FIRST POST PREVIEW
> [CLIPART: A roller rink with skeletons dancing]
>
> TECHNIQUE BREAKDOWN #1: Found Room, Generated Crowd
>
> Dr. Dreams' method, in his own words:
>
> "The roller rink in Bambi 9 is a real place. Moonlight Rollerway in Los Angeles. I comped the skeletons into a photo of it in Grok, then fed that composite back in as the background reference for the Seedance shot."
>
> The philosophy in five words: found room, generated crowd.
>
> THE ANCHOR: A photograph of an actual place. A roller rink that exists. Wax on the floor. Rental skates with broken buckles. This is the non-negotiable foundation.
>
> THE GENERATION: AI fills in what can't be filmed. Skeletons. Crowds. Heavy metal nightmares. Doowop sirens.
>
> THE LOOP: Composite → reference → generation → better composite. Each pass improves the anchor.
>
> THE ~ WUBU ~ VERSION: We have 4.95hr of found footage. We don't need to comp skeletons into a photo. We have the real room. We add the generated crowd.
>
> The room was found. Only the crowd was generated.
>
