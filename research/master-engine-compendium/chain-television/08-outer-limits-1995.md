## Core Facts

- **The Outer Limits (1995-2002, USA Network, 6 seasons, 155 episodes).** A revival of the 1963-1965 ABC series. Produced by **Leslie Belzman** (who created the revival). The series was a ratings success for USA Network and ran for 6 seasons — making it one of the most successful sci-fi anthology revivals of the 1990s.

- **The "Control Voice" was revived and updated.** The revival's Control Voice was narrated by **Len Cariou** (the actor, known for "Broadway's The Last Ship" and numerous TV roles). Cariou's voice was warmer than Vic Perrin's original but still retained the "control" tone. The updated Control Voice opening: "There is nothing wrong with your television set. Do not attempt to adjust the picture. We are controlling transmission. If we wish to make it louder, we will bring up the volume. If we wish to make it softer, we will tune it to a whisper. We will control the vertical. We will control the horizontal. We can roll the screen, make it ripple. We can change the focus to a soft blur or sharpen it to crystal clarity. For the next hour, sit quietly and we will control all that you see and hear. You are about to experience the awe and mystery which reaches from the inner mind to... The Outer Limits." (Similar to the original, with minor updates.)

- **Leslie Belzman** was the series creator/producer. Belzman was a television producer who had worked on many series before creating The Outer Limits revival. His role was to manage the production and maintain the franchise's identity while updating it for the 1990s.

- **Brent V. Friedman** was a writer/producer for the series. Friedman was a science fiction writer who had written for many shows. His episodes were among the most well-regarded of the revival. Friedman's style was more action-oriented than the original — the revival's episodes often had more physical conflict and less psychological tension.

- **Other notable writers:** **Story by** credits include many writers — the series was a writers' showcase. The revival had a rotating roster of writers, with Friedman being one of the most frequent contributors.

- **Episodes:** 155 episodes across 6 seasons. The series covered a wide range of sci-fi themes — aliens, time travel, parallel universes, technology gone wrong, biological horror. The revival was more action-oriented than the original, but still maintained the anthology format.

- **Reception:** The series was a ratings success for USA Network and developed a cult following. The revival was generally well-received by genre fans, though some critics felt it lacked the original's psychological depth.
