# NODE 20: Lost Media / Internet Archaeology — Mystery Community, Curation, Collective Memory

## Core concept
Lost media = the mystery/curation model: a community that excavates missing/corrupted/obscure media, preserves it, theorizes about it. The master engine's "memory/curation" mode.

## Key facts
- "Lost media" community: collective excavation of missing/corrupted/obscure media
- "Lost Media, and the Secrets of Digital Culture" (Nicole Lamerichs) — community is testament to audiences + their involvement with media; full of secrets, fan theories
- "Collective Re-Excavation and Lost Media from the Last Century of British Prehistoric Studies" (ResearchGate)
- "Web Archaeology: An Introduction" (TMG) — "digging" + "reconstructing" as central methods; 'memoryware' = software/hardware + crawlers/bots/curators/users through which web's history is documented AND constructed
- Internet archaeology = preserving + curating + theorizing digital cultural memory

## What this means for the engine
- The engine = a memory system: collecting, curating, archiving references
- The engine's "lost media" mode = excavating forgotten/obscure references and bringing them back
- The engine's community = the audience as co-curators (neokayfabe)
- The engine's archive = cultural memory: what's been referenced, what's been forgotten, what's been rediscovered
