## The Method for Finding Generic Descriptors

When you need to evoke a new format, use this method:
1. Watch 5-10 shows in the format (without naming them in your notes)
2. Identify the COMMON visual elements across all shows
3. Describe those common elements in generic terms
4. Test: could your description apply to any show in the format? If yes, it's generic. If no, it's too specific.
