## The Core Move
**Horror sets up dread; the Old UNC's deadpan defuses it.** The scare is real;
the reaction is the joke. Benign violation ([[HUMOR_BLUEPRINT]] Step 4) is the
whole engine.

## The Recipe (per horror-comedy bumper)
1. **Establish dread** (0-3s): shaky cam, dark liminal space, something off.
2. **Escalate the uncanny** (3-7s): walls breathe/expand, a face in the door,
   the VHS degrades.
3. **The UNC enters calm** (7-9s): he walks in, unimpressed.
4. **The button** (9-10s): he pats the wall / nods at the orb / sighs. "It's okay."
   The dread relaxes. Laugh = relief + absurdity.

## WuBu Horror Grammar (from research)
- **Analog Horror (Local 58):** institutional dread, found footage, text cards
  that lie. We use the *texture* (tape noise, prismatic beams) not the despair.
- **Lynch:** dream-logic, sound-as-mood (Badalamenti drones), the ordinary made
  wrong. We use the *wrongness*, not the menace.
- **Tales from the Crypt:** horror + comedy works when the humor is integral to
  the character. The UNC's deadpan IS the Crypt Keeper's pun.
- **British Dark Comedy:** the awful treated as Tuesday. The UNC treats the
  liminal as furniture.

## DO / DON'T
- DO: let the scare land first; the defuse is the payoff.
- DO: use VHS degradation as comedic timing (the glitch = the beat).
- DON'T: make it actually scary (then it's horror, not horror-comedy).
- DON'T: have the UNC react with fear (kills the benign violation).
