## The Thesis
**We are not making content. We are making context.**
Ted Turner didn't invent news — he invented CNN, the 24-hour news *context*.
We build the **~ WuBu ~ Television Universe**: a network of channels, shows,
and identities that are OURS. We absorb competitors' best ideas, make them
better, and claim the territory. As the Colonel said: *"What we propose to do
is not to control content, but to create context."*

## The 7 Originality Principles (governs every script)
1. **Create context, not control content** — the universe is the context.
2. **The room was found, only the crowd was generated** — anchor in real
   assets (4.95hr footage, 185 research files, 490 clipart, 297 voice videos);
   AI fills what can't be filmed.
3. **The Impostor is the Crewmate is the Impostor** — we are real, AI, bootleg,
   original. The ambiguity is the point; the audience fills the gaps.
4. **Selection for Societal Sanity** — we curate and filter (the Colonel's S3 Plan).
5. **Every Ponderable is worth picking up off the street** — no fluff, no filler.
6. **The Active Decoy works because it's hollow** — our personas/bootlegs draw
   fire so the real ~ WuBu ~ moves unseen.
7. **It's all information** — even fakes are data; everything feeds the compendium.
