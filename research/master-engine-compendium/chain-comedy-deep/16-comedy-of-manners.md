## Core Concept

Comedy of manners uses social conventions as the engine of comedy. Characters follow arbitrary social rules with absolute seriousness. The comedy comes from the gap between the rules' importance (to the characters) and their absurdity (to the audience).

## The WuBu Comedy of Manners

The ~ WuBu ~ height opposition world IS a comedy of manners:
- The social rules (tall vs. short) are arbitrary
- The characters follow them with absolute seriousness
- The audience sees the absurdity
- The Old UNC exists in both worlds, following neither set of rules
