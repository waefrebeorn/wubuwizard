## The Old UNC as Host

The Old UNC is the WuBuMovie's "host." He is not a game show host, not a Twilight Zone host, not a Crypt Keeper, not a Doctor Who narrator. He is a hybrid — the host of a found-footage anthology that is also a game show, also a horror show, also a comedy. The Old UNC's host persona is the "deadpan philosopher" — the gravelly voice, the flat stare, the dry humor, the uncertainty.

## The Old UNC's Host Persona — Synthesis

The Old UNC's host persona is a synthesis of these lessons:
1. **Serling (direct address):** The Old UNC addresses the audience directly. He looks into the camera. He speaks to the viewer.
2. **Control Voice (control framing):** The MEGA is the transmission. The Old UNC is experiencing it. The AGI is controlling it. The "control" framing is the WuBuMovie's framing.
3. **Whitaker (intimacy):** The Old UNC speaks as a friend, not a host. He shares a story, not a show.
4. **Crypt Keeper (personality as identity):** The Old UNC's personality — the stare, the staff, the hoody, the deadpan humor — is the show's identity.
5. **The Doctor (character as host):** The Old UNC is a character first, a host second. His personality is the show's identity.
6. **Trebek (audience trust through honesty):** The Old UNC's honesty (his uncertainty, his "I don't know what this is") is the audience's trust. The audience trusts him because he's not in control.

## WuBu Relevance

The Old UNC's host persona is the WuBuMovie's identity. The Old UNC is not a generic found-footage narrator.
