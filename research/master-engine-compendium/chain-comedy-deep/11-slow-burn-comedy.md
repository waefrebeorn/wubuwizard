## Core Concept

Slow burn comedy is the art of building tension slowly, then releasing it (or not releasing it) for maximum comedic effect. The audience is made to wait. The waiting IS the comedy. When the payoff comes, it's bigger because of the wait.

## The Structure

1. **Establish the tension** (0-5s): Something is wrong, but no one acknowledges it
2. **Deepen the tension** (5-8s): The wrong thing gets worse, still no acknowledgment
3. **Release or don't** (8-10s): Either the tension explodes (punchline) or it doesn't (anti-joke)

## The WuBu Slow Burn

The Old UNC is a slow burn character. He walks down a hallway. The hallway is breathing. He doesn't react. The audience waits for him to react. He doesn't. The bumper ends. The audience laughs because they were waiting for a reaction that never came.
