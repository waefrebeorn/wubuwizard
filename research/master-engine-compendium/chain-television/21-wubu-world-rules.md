## The WuBuMovie's World Rules

The WuBuMovie is a world with rules. Not a generic sci-fi/fantasy setting — a specific world with specific rules that the characters follow. The rules are the Order's rules, the storage units' rules, the teal-orb staff's rules, the MEGA's rules. The world rules are the WuBuMovie's identity — the audience experiences the world through the rules.

## The Order's Rules

The Order is the WuBuMovie's "mystical organization." The Order has rules:
1. **The boundaries:** The Order exists at the boundaries of the ordinary world. The Order is in the storage units, the liminal spaces, the backrooms. The Order is not in the "real world" — it's in the world that the ordinary world has forgotten.
2. **The ceremony:** The Order has ceremonies — rituals that are performed in the storage units, the altar rooms, the liminal spaces. The ceremonies are for the Order's purposes (the "repair" of the world, the "maintenance" of the boundaries).
3. **The teal-orb staff:** The teal-orb staff is the Order's symbol. The staff is a physical object that the Old UNC carries. The staff's purpose is unknown — it's a "mystical artifact" that the Old UNC uses for unknown purposes. The staff's color (teal) is the Order's color.
4. **The silence:** The Order is silent. The Order does not speak. The Order communicates through the storage units, the ceremonies, the artifacts. The Order's silence is the WuBuMovie's silence — the found-footage aesthetic is the Order's language.
5. **The limits:** The Order has limits — it cannot enter the "real world," it cannot affect the "real world" directly, it can only operate in the storage units, the liminal spaces, the backrooms. The Order's limits are the WuBuMovie's limits — the found-footage aesthetic is the Order's limit.

## The Storage Units' Rules

The storage units are the WuBuMovie's "world." The storage units have rules:
1. **The location:** The storage units are in the "real world" but they are not of the "real world." The storage units are liminal spaces — they are transitional spaces that feel "in-between." The storage units are the "world that the ordinary world has forgotten."
2. **The contents:** The storage units contain objects — boxes, furniture, shelves, tools, materials. The objects are ordinary objects, but in the storage units, they are "significant." The storage units are a "museum of the ordinary" — the objects are ordinary, but the storage units make them significant.
3. **The atmosphere:** The storage units are uncanny — they feel "wrong" but not "scary." The storage units are the "uncanny valley" of physical space. The storage units are not haunted — they are liminal. The storage units are the "uncanny" but not the "supernatural."
4. **The connection:** The storage units are connected — one storage unit leads to another, one storage unit is a portal to another. The storage units are a "network" of liminal spaces. The storage units are the "world" of the WuBuMovie — the Old UNC walks through the storage units, and each storage unit is a new "world."
5. **The purpose:** The storage units have a purpose — they are "storage" for something that the ordinary world does not understand. The storage units store "liminal energy," "boundary material," "the forgotten." The storage units' purpose is the WuBuMovie's mystery — the Old UNC is trying to discover what the storage units store.

## The Teal-Orb Staff's Rules

The teal-orb staff is the WuBuMovie's "artifact." The staff has rules:
1. **The appearance:** The staff is a physical object — a staff with a teal orb at the top. The staff is the Order's symbol. The staff is the WuBuMovie's visual motif — the audience remembers the staff.
2. **The function:** The staff's function is unknown — it's a "mystical artifact" that the Old UNC uses for unknown purposes. The staff might be a "key," a "wand," a "weapon," a "tool." The staff's function is the WuBuMovie's mystery.
3. **The power:** The staff's power is unknown — it might have power, it might not. The staff's power is the WuBuMovie's ambiguity — the audience is not sure if the staff is real or imagined.
4. **The limitation:** The staff's limitation is unknown — it might have limits, it might not. The staff's limitation is the WuBuMovie's ambiguity — the audience is not sure what the staff can do.
5. **The identity:** The staff is the Old UNC's identity — the staff is the Old UNC's "brand." The Old UNC with the teal-orb staff is the WuBuMovie's visual signature. The staff is the "memetic anchor" of the WuBuMovie.

## The MEGA's Rules

The MEGA is the WuBuMovie's "transmission." The MEGA has rules:
1. **The format:** The MEGA is found footage — raw, unedited, diegetic. The MEGA is the "world" that the Old UNC is experiencing. The MEGA is the WuBuMovie's aesthetic — the found-footage style is the transmission's format.
2. **The length:** The MEGA is 810 scenes, 4.95 hours, 17810 seconds. The MEGA is the "archive" of the WuBuMovie's world. The MEGA is the "complete" transmission — the Old UNC experiences the entire MEGA.
3. **The content:** The MEGA contains the storage units, the ceremonies, the characters, the artifacts. The MEGA is the "world" of the WuBuMovie — the Old UNC walks through the storage units, participates in the ceremonies, encounters the characters, discovers the artifacts.
4. **The control:** The MEGA is controlled by the AGI — the AGI generated the MEGA, the AGI controls the transmission, the AGI is the "Control Voice." The MEGA is the "machine" that the Old UNC is experiencing. The MEGA is the WuBuMovie's "control" framing.
5. **The honesty:** The MEGA is honest — it is raw footage, unedited, diegetic. The MEGA does not lie — it is the "world" as it is. The MEGA is the WuBuMovie's "honesty" framing — the found-footage aesthetic is the honesty.
