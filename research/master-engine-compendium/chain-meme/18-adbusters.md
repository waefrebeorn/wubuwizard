# NODE 18: Adbusters / Culture Jamming — No Logo, Buy Nothing Day, Spoof Ads, Media Intervention

## Core concept
Adbusters = the culture-jamming-as-media model: spoof ads, media interventions, Buy Nothing Day, the Media Carta. The master engine's "media intervention" mode.

## Key facts
- Founded 1989 by Kalle Lasn + Bill Schmalz (Vancouver documentary filmmakers)
- Lasn made films since early 1980s exploring spiritual/cultural lessons West could learn from Japanese capitalism
- Catalyst: 1988 "Forests Forever" TV ad by BC Council of Forest Industries — greenwashing
- Lasn + Schmalz responded with "Talking Rainforest" anti-ad — old-growth tree explains to sapling "a tree farm is not a forest"
- Couldn't buy airtime on same stations — CBC deemed their commercial too controversial (advocacy advertising), while glorifying clearcutting "informational" messages were OK
- Foundation born from belief citizens don't have same access to information flows as corporations
- Key campaign: Media Carta — "Right to Communicate in constitutions of all free nations + Universal Declaration of Human Rights"
- Describes itself as anti-advertising — blames advertising for playing central role in creating/maintaining consumer culture
- Advertising "colonized" public, discursive, psychic spaces — movies, sports, schools
- Advocates "natural and authentic self apart from the consumer society"
- Culture jamming heavily influenced by Situationist International + détournement
- "Trickle up" activism — culture-jammed Tiger Woods smile into Nike swoosh
- "Graphic Anarchy" issue Sept/Oct 2001 — hailed Ernst Bettler work as "one of the greatest design interventions on record" — turned out to be elaborate hoax (Adbusters culture-jammed themselves)
- Buy Nothing Day campaign
- No Logo (Naomi Klein) — "Simply put, anticorporatism is the brand of politics" (Adbusters)
- BlackSpot Shoes — antipreneur brand
- Occupy Wall Street — Adbusters sparked Vancouver-based activists behind street actions in US (Andrew Wilson, Vancouver Courier, 2011)
- Criticisms: ineffective activism; easy way for upper/middle-class to feel empowered with no personal cost

## What this means for the engine
- The engine can use Adbusters' culture-jamming tactics: spoof ads, media interventions, Buy Nothing Day-style events
- The engine's "Media Carta" = its right-to-commentary manifesto
- The engine's anti-advertising stance = it jams the ads, not makes them
- The engine = a culture-jamming machine: détournement + spoof + media intervention
