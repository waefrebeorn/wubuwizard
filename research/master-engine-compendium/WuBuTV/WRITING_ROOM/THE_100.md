# THE 100 — the full network slate

Written 2026-09-26. 100 episodes across 9 canonical channels + Network/Archive.
Every entry is **shootable as written**: a load-bearing detail, a silent-frame
gamble, and a one-line prompt seed. Nothing here needs a premise re-derived.

**The four rules every one of these obeys:**
1. The world refuses to explain itself. Someone is completely calm about it.
2. It must read from a **silent frame**. If it needs dialogue to land, it isn't ready.
3. **One load-bearing detail** per episode, named. It must hold across a 6-tile.
4. Landscape 16:9, subject **centred**, `~ WuBu ~` bug, native audio only.

**Already shot (do not re-shoot):** S-1..S-7 · N-1, N-2 · O-1 · PT-1, PT-2 ·
B-ident t1/t2/t3 + sign-on.

## PRODUCTION NOTES FOR THE WHOLE 100
- **The 6-tile is the gate.** Every load-bearing detail named above gets a tile.
- **A contact sheet is not a drift check.** Ten frames across 30s will make a
  camera pan look like a set break. It nearly cost PT-1 a reroll.
- **Batch by channel.** Cast and set are stable within a channel, so continuity
  cost is lowest there. Switching channels every episode maximises drift.
- **A still is not a QC pass.** A still of an open mouth reads as an emotion.
- **No episode needs a licence.** If the model produces one, reject — and the
  rejection reason is "a face in a place faces do not belong," which is why
  detail-native homes matter.
