# THE CONTROL VOICE — WuBuTV network sign-on and anthology

Built 2026-09-26 from the research depot: `chain-television/05-outer-limits-1963-L2.md`
already contained this framing written for us. It was sitting in the depot
untransmitted and unbuilt. This is it, made shootable.

**Why this channel exists.** WuBuTV had nine channels and no sign-on. A network
that has never introduced itself is a list of programmes, not a network. The
Outer Limits' Control Voice is the device that fixes that: not a warm presenter,
but a cold authority that frames the schedule and is never explained.

**The three rules that make it WuBuTV and not a copy:**
1. **The Control Voice is never named on screen.** It is a voice and a letterhead.
2. **It is never hostile.** It is administrative. The horror is that it is calm.
3. **UNC never acknowledges it.** A character acknowledging the network by name
   is a bug — the same class as him acknowledging the other channels.

## THE SIGN-ON — 40s, cold open

```
CONTROL VOICE: There is nothing wrong with your set.
CONTROL VOICE: Do not attempt to adjust the picture.
CONTROL VOICE: We are controlling transmission.
CONTROL VOICE: For the next 4.95 hours, sit quietly and we will control
               all that you see and hear.
CONTROL VOICE: You are about to experience the awe and mystery which reaches
               from the inner mind to the shelves.
CONTROL VOICE: This is the WuBu ~ network.
CONTROL VOICE: Nine channels. One building. No further explanation is available.
```

Read flat, unhurried, and slightly too close to the microphone. No music bed
under the opening two lines. **The name is spoken once and never again.**

### CLOSING STING — 12s
```
CONTROL VOICE: You have just watched a transmission from the archives.
CONTROL VOICE: The Control Voice is always watching.
CONTROL VOICE: Transmission ends. The building does not.
```

## THE TWIST FORMULA (EC, per chain-television/16)

Every anthology episode uses the same durable shape:

**ordinary people + an unexplained element + a twist that reveals the
character's true relationship to the building.**

The twist is never the monster. The twist is that the person was always part of
the paperwork. Premise 32 again: the room was real, the crowd was generated, and
this is the paperwork.

# CV-1 — THE CORRIDOR DOES NOT LOOP
```
CHANNEL:     control_voice   |   FORMAT: anthology   |   ENGINE: observation
JOKE TYPE:   observation     |   RECURRING BIT: the building as an archive
CHARACTER:   Old UNC         |   DURATION: 30s
```

**BEAT SHEET**
```
[0:00-0:10] SETUP:   Empty corridor, no one in it. He walks the row slowly,
                   counting doors under his breath.
[0:10-0:20] DEEPEN:  He reaches the far wall, turns around, walks back. The
                   count is longer. He does not look surprised. He looks like a
                   man checking a figure he already expected.
[0:20-0:30] PUNCHLINE: He writes the number on the clipboard. It is the same
                   number he wrote at the start. The corridor has not changed.
                   The corridor is not the variable.
TAG:           Never explained. Not by him, not by a card, not by the Control
               Voice.
```

**GENERATION PROMPT**
```
Hand-painted 2D cartoon, thick black outlines, flat comic palette, 1970s
public-access broadcast, VHS chroma bleed, scanlines.

SUBJECTS: a heavyset Black man with a large rounded afro, thick mustache and
goatee, wearing a BLACK hoodie with WuBu in YELLOW/GOLD letters. A storage
corridor: corrugated steel unit doors, stencilled block numbers, grey carpet,
fluorescent tubes where exactly ONE runs a different colour temperature. A metal
clipboard in his hand with a single column of plain block NUMBERS.

TIMELINE:
[0s-10s] Wide. He walks the row left to right, doors passing, mouth moving as
        though counting. He does not touch the doors.
[10s-20s] He reaches the far end, turns around, and walks back the other way.
         Same set, same doors, same one odd tube.
[20s-30s] He writes a number on the clipboard. He looks at it. He looks at the
         clipboard again. His expression does not change. Cut to end.

CAMERA: locked off wide for the first walk; ONE slow pan following him on the
return, and nothing else. No cuts, no push in, no reframe.

AUDIO: continuous low fluorescent room tone hum, 0s to 30s. At 4s soft
footsteps on carpet. At 13s footsteps again, reverse direction. At 22s a pen on
clipboard, one short scratch. Nothing else. No music, no voice, no SFX not
listed here.

LOAD-BEARING DETAIL: the stencilled unit numbers must be IDENTICAL in every
frame, and the ONE odd tube must be the SAME tube. If the corridor changes at
all, the joke dies. 6-tile the number strip and the light row.

SILENT-FRAME GAMBLE: a man counts a corridor twice, gets a different answer, and
writes down the first one without blinking. Reads with the sound off.

TEXT: diegetic timecode bottom-left only. The clipboard numbers are objects in
the world, not graphics. NO title card, no lower-third, no ident sting.

CANON: identity lock, restate every time - a heavyset Black man with a large
rounded afro, thick mustache and goatee, wearing a BLACK hoodie with WuBu in
YELLOW/GOLD letters. Wardrobe does not change. Set: storage corridor, one tube
at a different colour temperature. Load-bearing: unit numbers and the odd tube,
unchanged.

NEGATIVE: no soft dissolves, no subtitles, no narration, no watermark other than
the two-line corner bug, no invented text, no letter-shaped noise, no licensed
cartoon characters, no franchise faces, no animal mascots, no real people, no
real logos, no smile, no surprise, no reaction, no gasp, no music, no stinger,
no explanation of any kind.
```

```

**BEAT SHEET**
```

                   figure aloud, flatly. The audience seats are all empty.
[0:10-0:20] DEEPEN:  He turns the page. The same seats are now FULL - identical

                   count. The spheres do not move. He closes the clipboard.
TAG:           Premise 32, structural: the room was real, the crowd was

               comment on it. It never comments on anything.

# CV-3 — THE CLOSING OF THE ARCHIVE
```
CHANNEL:     control_voice   |   FORMAT: anthology   |   ENGINE: misdirection
JOKE TYPE:   misdirection (the archive closes; the building is the archive)
RECURRING BIT: the building as archive   |   CHARACTER: Old UNC   |   DURATION: 30s
```

**BEAT SHEET**
```
[0:00-0:10] SETUP:   He stands at the end of a corridor, clipboard in hand. The
                   lights are off behind him. He is looking at a single lit bay.
[0:10-0:20] DEEPEN:  He reads a figure, then closes the clipboard. He does not
                   switch the light off. He walks away from the lit bay and out
                   of frame, and the light stays on.
[0:20-0:30] PUNCHLINE: The camera holds on the empty, lit bay with nobody in it.
                   The light is still on. Nothing resolves. The building keeps
                   the record open.
TAG:           He never closes anything. The archive is the building; the
               building is not a room he can lock.
```
