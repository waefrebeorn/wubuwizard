# NODE 16: Know Your Meme Deep — Jamie Wilkinson Lineage, Meme Taxonomy, Viral Format Studies

## Core concept
Know Your Meme = the engine's memory layer: a taxonomy of memes, viral formats, internet culture. The master engine needs its own KYM — a reference system for what's viral, what's dead, what's mutating.

## Key facts
- KYM founded Dec 2007 as Rocketboom video series by Kenyatta Cheese, Elspeth Rountree, Jamie Wilkinson + CEO Andrew Baron
- "Rocketboom Entity for Internet Studies" — lab coats, "meme experts"
- Wilkinson had personal database of internet memes → repurposed as companion website, launched 2008
- By 2010 KYM more popular than original web series
- Jamie Wilkinson = lead developer of expanded database at end of 2008
- As of Jan 2017: 2,700+ "confirmed" meme entries
- Categories: confirmed, submitted, deadpooled (rejected), researching, popular
- Described by Daily Dot + WSJ as "the Encyclopedia Britannica of memes and internet culture"
- Time: "50 Best Websites 2009"
- Streamy Award 2010; Webby Award 2012
- June 2014: inducted into Web Archiving Program of American Folklife Center, Library of Congress
- Acquired by Cheezburger Network March 2011; Cheezburger acquired by Literally Media 2016
- Vice interview: "A Co-Creator of Know Your Meme Explains What the Hell a Meme Actually Is"
- Dembot: "The History of Know Your Meme" — Jamie Wilkinson as lead dev, KYM about "explicating memes and establishing a vocabulary"

## What this means for the engine
- The engine needs its OWN KYM — a meme/reference taxonomy
- Categories: confirmed (active), submitted (pending), deadpooled (dead), researching (in-progress), popular (trending)
- The engine's ingestion layer = KYM mode: track memes, formats, viral patterns
- The engine's memory = the KYM database: what's been referenced, what's been invaded, what's been deadpooled
