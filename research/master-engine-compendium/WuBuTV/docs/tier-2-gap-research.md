### Festival Types
| Type | Examples | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Top Tier** | Sundance, Cannes, TIFF, Berlinale | Career-making (hard to get into) |
| **Genre** | Fantastic Fest, Sitges, Fantasia, FrightFest | Horror/fantasy (our best bet) |

1. **Fantastic Fest** (Austin) — horror/fantasy, genre-focused

4. **FrightFest** (London) — horror-specific

### Submission Strategy
1. **Start local** → build credibility
2. **Target genre festivals** → horror/comedy fit
3. **Premiere status matters** → don't post online before festival
4. **Cover letter** → personalize, keep to one page
5. **Press kit** → synopsis, director statement, stills, poster, trailer
6. **Ask for waivers** → many festivals offer discount codes
7. **Network** → reach out to selection committee members
8. **Submit early** → early bird deadlines are cheaper

## 2. VOICE ACTING & NARRATION

### The Old UNC's Voice
The Old UNC has a "gravelly deadpan philosopher" voice. Options:
1. **AI voice generation** (ElevenLabs, etc.) — consistent, controllable
2. **Human voice actor** — more authentic, harder to control
3. **Text on screen** (current approach) — no voice needed, more mysterious

### AI Voice Tools (2026)
| Tool | Quality | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ElevenLabs** | Excellent | $5-330/mo | Most realistic, voice cloning |
| **Murf.ai** | Good | $19-99/mo | Business-focused |
| **Play.ht** | Good | $39-99/mo | Ultra-realistic |
| **Edge TTS** | Decent | Free | Built into Hermes already |

### Voice Acting Principles
- **Deadpan** = minimal inflection, slight pauses, dry delivery
- **Pacing** = slower than normal speech, let the silence work
- **Volume** = consistent, slightly quieter than "normal" (draws viewer in)
- **Microphone** = close-mic'd, slight bass boost for "gravelly" sound

### Recommendation
For now, **text on screen** is the right choice. It's more mysterious, more memeable, and doesn't require voice consistency. When we need spoken lines, use Edge TTS (free, already available) or ElevenLabs.

## 3. COPYRIGHT & FAIR USE

### The Basics
- **Copyright** protects creative works (music, video, images, characters)
- **Trademark** protects brand identity (names, logos, trade dress)
- **Fair use** allows limited use for commentary, criticism, parody

### Music in Video
| Scenario | Risk | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Using copyrighted music | Content ID claim, mute, or takedown | Use original music or licensed tracks |
| Using our own music (cleveland_video/) | None — we own it | Distribute via DistroKid + Content ID |
| Cover songs | Mechanical license needed | Avoid or license through DistroKid |
| Fair use (parody/commentary) | Gray area, platform-dependent | Transformative use is key |

### Protecting ~ WuBu ~ IP
1. **Register copyright** for the MEGA, bumpers, music (copyright.gov)
2. **Trademark** the ~ WuBu ~ name and logo (USPTO)
3. **YouTube Content ID** — register our music so others can't use it
4. **Watermark** — ~ WuBu ~ watermark on all content

### Fair Use for Parody
~ WuBu ~ bumpers parody TV formats. This is protected under fair use IF:
- The use is transformative (adds new meaning)
- Only as much of the original is necessary
- Doesn't compete with the original

**Our bootleg grammar IS the fair use strategy** — we describe visual grammar, not brand names.

## 4. BLENDER BASICS (Kane Parsons Used It)

### Why Blender?
Kane Parsons made Backrooms with Blender (free, open source). We might need it for:
- 3D environments (liminal spaces, storage units)
- Character modeling (the Old UNC, teal orbs)
- Animation (title cards, transitions)
- VFX (smoke, particles, lighting)

### The Learning Path
1. **Blender Guru Donut Tutorial** — the classic first project (4-6 hours)
2. **Blender fundamentals** — interface, navigation, modeling
3. **Materials and lighting** — making things look good
4. **Animation basics** — keyframes, timing, camera movement
5. **Compositing** — combining 3D with live footage

### Do We Actually Need Blender?
**Not right now.** Our AI video tools (when we get them) can generate environments. Blender is for when we need PRECISE control over 3D spaces. Kane used it because Backrooms requires impossible architecture. Our ~ WuBu ~ world is more about atmosphere than architecture.

**Priority: LOW.** Research it when we need to build specific 3D environments that AI can't generate.
