# NODE 12: Situationist International — Détournement, Culture Jamming, Spectacle, Guy Debord

## Core concept
SI = the theoretical foundation for the engine's entire commentary/irony system: détournement (turning expressions of the capitalist system against itself), the spectacle, culture jamming.

## Key facts
- 1957-1972, international org of social revolutionaries — avant-garde artists, intellectuals, political theorists
- Guy Debord = de facto leader and theorist
- Founders: Debord, Asger Jorn, Walter Olmo, Giuseppe Pinot-Gallizio, Piero Simondo, Elena Verrone, Michèle Bernstein
- Methods: Dérive, Détournement, Situlogy (Hypergraphy)
- Détournement: "turning expressions of the capitalist system against itself" — turning slogans/logos against advertisers/political status quo
- "The literary and artistic heritage of humanity should be used for partisan propaganda purposes... We must now push this process to the point of negating the negation" (Debord + Wolman, 1956)
- "There is no Situationist art, only Situationist uses of art"
- Spectacle: "not the domination of the world by images... but the domination of a social interaction mediated by images" (Debord, Society of the Spectacle, 1967)
- "All real relationships having been replaced by that of relationships with commodities"
- Society of the Spectacle: "written with the deliberate intention of doing harm to spectacular society"
- Legacy: influenced Fluxus, Neoism, Mark Divo; punk rock (Malcolm McLaren, Jamie Reid, Factory Records, Feederz); Sex Pistols; anarchist movements (King Mob, Weathermen, Rebel Worker); Adbusters, Reclaim the Streets
- Abbie Hoffman's pranks as counter-spectacle: penthouse levitation, NYSE money drop, Pigasus nomination; humor as counter-spectacle; danger of recuperation

## What this means for the engine
- Détournement = the engine's core tactic: turn expressions of the system against itself
- The spectacle = the engine's target: a social interaction mediated by images
- "No Situationist art, only Situationist uses of art" = the engine doesn't make art, it USES art as commentary
- The engine = a Situationist machine: détournement + counter-spectacle + culture jamming
