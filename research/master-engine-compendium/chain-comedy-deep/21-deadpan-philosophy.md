## Core Concept

The deadpan character is a philosophical position: the world is absurd, and the only rational response is to not react. Buster Keaton, Leslie Nielsen, and the Old UNC all share this philosophy. The deadpan character is the audience's surrogate — they see the absurdity but don't break character.

## The Rules

1. **Never react**: The deadpan character treats the absurd as normal
2. **Never explain**: The deadpan character doesn't acknowledge the joke
3. **Never break**: The deadpan character maintains their composure no matter what
4. **The rare break IS the comedy**: When the Old UNC sighs, it's funnier because he never reacts
