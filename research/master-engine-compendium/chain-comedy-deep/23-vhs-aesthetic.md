## Core Concept

The VHS aesthetic — scan lines, chroma bleed, tape noise, CRT curvature — is not just a visual style. It's a comedy tool. The degradation signals "this is real, this was found, this is not polished." The audience's brain reads the degradation as authenticity. That authenticity makes the absurd content funnier because it's presented as "real."

## The Elements

- **Scan lines**: Horizontal lines across the image (CRT monitor artifact)
- **Chroma bleed**: Colors bleeding into each other (tape degradation)
- **Tape noise**: Hiss, warble, tracking errors (magnetic tape degradation)
- **CRT curvature**: The slight curve of an old TV screen
- **Tracking lines**: Horizontal bands of distortion that roll through the image
