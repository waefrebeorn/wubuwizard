## WEAKNESS 2: We Don't Understand Distribution

### The Problem
We have 9 channels planned but no idea how to actually get views. Making content ≠ getting watched.

### What We Don't Know (Now Learned)
- **TikTok algorithm 2026**: 70%+ completion rate required (up from 50% in 2024)
- **Follower-first distribution**: Your followers are the test audience. If they don't engage, your video dies.
- **Rewatch rate**: One viewer watching 3x beats three viewers watching once
- **Original audio gets priority**: Trending sounds work, but original audio gets a boost
- **TikTok is a search engine**: 64% of Gen Z searches on TikTok like Google
- **Engagement velocity**: Fast engagement in first hour = wider reach
- **Longer videos winning**: 60-180s outperforming 15s (but our bumpers are 10s — that's fine for bumpers, not for main content)

### What We Need to Learn
1. **Hook writing**: First 3 seconds determine 80% of viral potential
2. **Caption SEO**: Keywords people search for, not just descriptions
3. **Posting schedule**: When our audience is online, consistency
4. **Cross-platform strategy**: Same content adapted for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, X

### The Viral Formula (From Research)
| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Emotional Trigger** | Joy, surprise, awe, anger — high-arousal emotions get shared |
| **High Relatability** | "Oh my god, that's so me" — makes people share |
| **Timing & Relevance** | Tap into current trends, cultural moments |
| **Algorithmic Fit** | High watch time, shares, completion rate |

### The Sharing Psychology
- **High-arousal emotions** (joy, shock, anger) get shared. **Low-arousal** (sadness, contentment) gets likes but not shares.
- **Social currency**: People share things that make them look smart, funny, helpful, in-the-know
- **Emotional contagion**: Make someone feel something intense → they NEED to pass it on
