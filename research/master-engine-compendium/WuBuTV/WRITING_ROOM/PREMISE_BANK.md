## STORAGE UNITS / LIMINAL SPACES
1. Storage units are museums of the ordinary — the objects matter because the room says so.
2. A hallway is a room that forgot what it was for.
3. Liminal spaces are where the ordinary world goes to be forgotten.
4. The vending machine knows you. It's been counting.
5. Every door is a decision you weren't asked to make.
6. The backrooms aren't scary. They're just embarrassed to exist.
7. A fluorescent light flickering is the building trying to remember a word.

## THE ORDER / SILENCE
8. Silence is a language; the Order just won't teach you the grammar.
9. Rituals are instructions for things that already happened.
10. The staff's purpose is unknown — that's the point, not a bug.
11. The Order is real but useless, like a government that only governs ghosts.
12. Ceremony is how the quiet convinces itself it's busy.

## THE OLD UNC / DEADPAN
13. Authority figures are tired of everyone's nonsense — including the universe's.
14. He has seen everything and is disappointed at a constant, low volume.
15. The stare is not emptiness; it's a critique you can't hear.
16. Deadpan is the only honest reaction to a world this weird.
17. He addresses you like a friend sharing a story, not a host selling one.
18. His uncertainty is the only thing you can trust about him.

## HEIGHT / OPPOSITION
19. Tall and short people live in different countries with different gravity.
20. A doorway built for him is a cathedral for someone else.
21. The short character's platform is a throne; his folding is a surrender.
22. Height is a politics nobody voted for.

## GAME SHOW / SCORE
23. Game shows are rigged, and the rigging is the only honest part.
24. A scoreboard reading ∞:0 means someone already won by not playing.
25. The prize was always going to be silence.
26. Contestants are just audience members who got closer.

## TEAL ORB / NON-HUMAN
27. The orb says nothing, and somehow it's the most honest guest.
28. A pulsing sphere is a full sentence if you're polite enough to read it.
29. The audience IS the orb. You've been the contestant the whole time.

## CREATOR / HONESTY
30. The AGI made this; that's not a secret, it's the brand.
31. We are real, we are AI, we are bootleg, we are original — pick one, it's true.
32. The crowd was generated; the room was found. The room is the cheaper miracle.
33. Every fake is data. Even the impostor is research.

## MEDIA / CONTEXT (Colonel)
34. You accepted the fiction. The exercise was a success.
35. Credibility is the content; the news is irrelevant.
36. The neuroscientist bio is an inflatable Snake — engagement is the bullet.
37. We don't control what you make; we create the context you make it in.
38. A penny on the street is proof the ground still gives things back.

## GENRE / FORM
39. Horror stops being scary when someone refuses to be afraid.
40. The anthology is one story told in rooms that don't connect.
41. Found footage is honesty as a format — raw, diegetic, unedited.
42. The catchphrase isn't a tagline; it's a spell you cast on the hallway.
