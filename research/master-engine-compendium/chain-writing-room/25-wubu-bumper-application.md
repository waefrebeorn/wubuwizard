## The Old UNC: The Bumper Character

The Old UNC is the primary bumper vehicle. His attributes:
- **Deadpan philosopher** — flat stare, dry humor, gravelly presence
- **Teal-orb staff** — his signature prop, appears in every format
- **~WuBu~ hoody** — his uniform, the brand on his body
- **Liminal spaces** — his natural environment (hallways, storage units, vending-machine rooms)
- **Height** — he's tall, which creates the height opposition dynamic

In bumper scripts, the Old UNC is the constant. The format changes around him; he doesn't change. That's the comedy: the world shifts, he doesn't.

## The Height Opposition World

The ~ WuBu ~ universe has a built-in contrast engine: height as a political/social category. Tall characters and short characters occupy different spaces, have different rules, different power dynamics.

In bumper scripts, the height opposition is a visual joke that needs no setup. The audience sees a tall character and a short character in the same frame and the joke is already halfway there. The writing room's job is to push it further:
- The short character has a tall platform. The Old UNC takes a step back (now he's "shorter" via perspective).
- A doorway that's the right height for the Old UNC is a cathedral for the short character.
- The game show podium is adjusted for the short character. The Old UNC has to fold in half.

The height opposition is treated as a *world rule*, not a one-off gag. It's always there, always consistent, always escalating.

## The Format Catalog

The ~ WuBu ~ Television Universe has nine channels, each with its own bumper format:

### 1. WuBu NEWS (news desk format)
- **Engine:** Contrast (serious news format + absurd stories)
- **Joke types:** Observation, Exaggeration, POV
- **Example:** "Today's top story: a hallway in Sector 7 has stopped breathing. The Old UNC has been called in. More at 11. There is no 11."

### 2. WuBu ORIGINALS (found footage format)
- **Engine:** Misdirection + Absurd
- **Joke types:** Misdirection, Absurdity, Act-Out
- **Example:** Old UNC walks down a hallway. The hallway is breathing. He doesn't react. The hallway breathes faster. He stops. It stops. He walks. It breathes. He sighs.

### 3. WuBu SATIRE (game show format)
- **Engine:** Contrast (game show enthusiasm + Old UNC deadpan)
- **Joke types:** Exaggeration, Contrast, POV
- **Example:** "WELCOME TO: Who Wants to Understand the Old UNC!" Contestant answers. Old UNC stares. Buzzer. "Incorrect. The correct answer was silence."

### 4. WuBu LABS (infomercial format)
- **Engine:** Absurd + Contrast
- **Joke types:** Exaggeration, Wordplay, Analogy
- **Example:** "Tired of hallways that don't breathe? Try HALLWAY-AID." [Old UNC sprays something. Hallway sighs.] "Available in stores. There are no stores."

### 5. WuBu ARENA (sports/arena format)
- **Engine:** Contrast (height opposition as sport)
- **Joke types:** Act-Out, Exaggeration, Contrast
- **Example:** Two characters of different heights compete in an event. The event is "who can look more disappointed." The Old UNC judges. He gives both a 10. They're both disappointed in his judging.

### 6. WuBu SITCOM (three-wall sitcom format)
- **Engine:** Contrast (sitcom normalcy + ~ WuBu ~ weirdness)
- **Joke types:** Observation, Callback, Act-Out
- **Example:** Old UNC on a couch. Another character: "You ever notice the hallway watches you?" Old UNC: "Every day." [Laugh track. The hallway blushes.]

### 7. WuBu HORROR (found footage / horror format)
- **Engine:** Misdirection (horror setup + comedy payoff)
- **Joke types:** Misdirection, Absurdity, POV
- **Example:** Shaky cam. Dark hallway. Something's breathing. The Old UNC walks into frame, completely calm. The breathing stops, startled. He pats the wall. "It's okay." The wall relaxes.

### 8. WuBu TALK (talk show format)
- **Engine:** Contrast (talk show format + absurd guest)
- **Joke types:** Analogy, Wordplay, Observation
- **Example:** Old UNC interviews a teal orb. The orb says nothing. Old UNC: "Strong points. Would you say you're more of a morning orb or an evening orb?" Orb pulses once. Old UNC: "I respect that."

### 9. WuBu COMMERCIAL (commercial format)
- **Engine:** Absurd + Exaggeration
- **Joke types:** Exaggeration, Wordplay, Analogy
- **Example:** "Introducing: WuBu BRAND STORAGE UNITS. Each unit contains a different dimension. [Old UNC opens unit. Galaxy inside.] Dimensions may vary. No refunds. There is no 'back.'"

## The South Park "Balls in a Tank" Philosophy (Applied)

The operating principle: throw a LOT of different funny things into the same 10-second space, and let them collide.

In practice, this means:
- **The Old UNC** is one funny thing (his deadpan in a weird world)
- **The format** is another funny thing (game show, sitcom, horror)
- **The world** is another funny thing (breathing hallways, opinionated vending machines)
- **The height opposition** is another funny thing (when a second character is present)

Four funny things in 10 seconds. They collide. The audience laughs at the collisions.

## The Old UNC Bumper Scripts (What They Are NOT)

The old bumper scripts (the three from the previous era) described shots. "The Old UNC stares at a vending machine. Cut to the vending machine. Cut back to the Old UNC." That's not a joke. That's a description of what the camera sees.

The writing room research teaches you to write JOKES in 10 seconds. The bumper isn't "here's a scene of the Old UNC staring" — it's "here's the Old UNC staring, and then something funny happens, and then another thing, and then the whole thing pivots."

The difference:
- **Old:** The Old UNC stares at a vending machine. (No joke. Just a shot.)
- **New:** The Old UNC stares at a vending machine. The machine dispenses a chicken. He takes it. Text: "Day 47." (Joke: the machine knows him, this happens every day, the chicken is a callback.)
