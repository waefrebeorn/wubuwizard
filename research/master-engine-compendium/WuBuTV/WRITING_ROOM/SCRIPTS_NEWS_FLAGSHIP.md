# WUBUNEWS — FLAGSHIP SCRIPT SET (N-5 … N-10)

Six News episodes, written 2026-09-29. Built from the research depot
(`chain-commentary`, `chain-meme`, `chain-television`) rather than from the
existing slate, because the slate's News entries were one-liners and these are
full seven-block prompts.

**The News channel's grammar, from `chain-commentary/13-heathcote-williams` and
`chain-commentary/12-situationist-international`:** a deadpan anchor delivering
information that is technically correct and contextually insane. The joke is
never in a false statement — it is in a *true* statement delivered without
acknowledging why it is strange. The channel does not wink. Nobody in it is
worried.

**Load-bearing rule for this whole set:** whatever the chyron says must be
**legible and unchanged** in every frame. It is the only text in the shot, so
it is the thing a generator will corrupt. Every chyron is short, in block caps,
and describes exactly what is on screen — never a joke, never a wink.

## WHY THESE SIX

The slate's News entries were one-line premises. These are finished episodes, and
each one is the **same joke in a different key**: an accurate report of something
impossible, delivered without a flicker.

- N-5 the report never claims the spheres are people
- N-6 the tape is unbroken and always was
- N-7 the statistic is true and the two figures disagree
- N-8 the evidence is present, tidy, and explains nothing
- N-9 the correction confirms the original
- N-10 the record is complete, which is why there is nothing to report

**One set, one desk, one costume.** Cut them as a single run. The only variable
between episodes is the object on the desk, which is exactly how a real channel
would shoot a week of one story.

**Cross-scene check required** before hand-off, against n4-t1 as the News
reference set — N-6's monitor corridor and N-10's shelf are both places that can
drift from the building's actual geography.
