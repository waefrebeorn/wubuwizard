# NODE 24: Fernand Braudel — Longue Durée, Civilization Threefold Time, Mediterranean, Deep History

## Core concept
Fernand Braudel = the three-clock model of historical time: longue durée (environmental), medium time (economies/cultures), short time (events). The master engine's "time-scale operating system."

## Key facts
- Fernand Braudel (1902-1985) — French historian, key figure in Annales school, leader post-WWII
- Most significant contribution: THREE-TIERED VIEW OF HISTORICAL TIME:
  1. LONGUE DURÉE — environmental time, practically immobile, geo-history; the long term; slow-changing geographic factors, constraints of natural environment on human production + communication
  2. Medium time — economies, societies, cultures; conjunctures (periods of decades or centuries when more profound cultural changes like industrial revolution can take place)
  3. Short time — discrete events (histoire événementielle); the subject of chroniclers + journalists
- "The history of the event (histoire événementielle), the conjuncture, and the longue durée" — Medium article
- "All the human sciences, history included, are contaminated by one another. They speak the same language, or can do so" (Braudel 1958:731)
- Conceived the three-tiered time while in captivity (1944) — 5 years in POW camps in Mainz + Lübeck; phenomenal memory; drafted La Méditerranée et le monde méditerranéen à l'époque de Philippe II (1949)
- "Total history" — relied heavily on quantification + dazzling microstudies of villages and regions
- Major works: "The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World in the Age of Philip II" (1949); "Civilization and Capitalism, 15th-18th Century"; "The Identity of France" (unfinished, final work)
- Shifted historical focus from political leaders/diplomacy/wars to ordinary people, climate, demography, agriculture, social dynamics
- Mountains vs plains dwellers — different cultures + economic models — as basic feature of Mediterranean history over thousands of years (first volume of The Mediterranean)
- Mentor: Lucien Febvre; taught at University of São Paolo (1935-37); founded Maison des Sciences de l'Homme (1962)
- Britannica: "Braudel's concept of historical time recognizes that human experience occurs on different time scales, each with its own speed and impact, leaving physical and mental traces"

## What this means for the engine
- The engine's time-scale operating system = Braudel's three clocks:
  - Longue durée: the engine's deep-time layer (civilization, environment, cosmology)
  - Medium time: the engine's cultural layer (economies, societies, cultures)
  - Short time: the engine's event layer (news, memes, daily commentary)
- The engine operates on ALL THREE CLOCKS SIMULTANEOUSLY
- The engine's "total history" = Braudel's total history: quantification + microstudies
- The engine = a Braudelian machine: three clocks + total history + deep time
