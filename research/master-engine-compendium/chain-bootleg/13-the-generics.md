## The Generic Descriptor System

"The Generics" is the complete system of non-trademarked visual descriptors that the writing room uses to build prompts. It's organized into six categories:

### 1. Color Generics
- Warm: amber, gold, cream, wood tones, soft yellow
- Cool: blue, teal, steel gray, fluorescent white
- High contrast: black and white, stark, graphic
- Muted: desaturated, faded, pastel, washed-out
- Vibrant: saturated, colorful, bright, primary colors

### 2. Lighting Generics
- Sitcom: warm, ambient, studio, soft shadows
- Game show: bright, even, colorful, high-key
- Horror: dim, flickering, single-source, harsh shadows
- Found footage: available light, overexposed, harsh flash
- Talk show: spotlight, soft fill, warm key
- News: bright, even, professional, blue-white

### 3. Framing Generics
- Sitcom: medium shot, slightly high angle, three-shot
- Game show: wide shot, low angle, crowd shot
- Horror: tight framing, Dutch angle, POV shot
- Found footage: handheld, shaky, accidental framing
- Talk show: medium close-up, eye-level, two-shot
- News: medium shot, eye-level, direct address

### 4. Camera Generics
- Sitcom: static, occasional pan, snap zoom
- Game show: dolly in, crane shot, push-in
- Horror: slow push, static, handheld shake
- Found footage: handheld, operator movement
- Talk show: static, slow push-in
- News: static, occasional pan

### 5. Texture Generics
- Analog: VHS degradation, scan lines, chroma bleed
- Digital: clean, sharp, compressed
- Film: grain, soft focus, light leaks
- Institutional: cinderblock, linoleum, fluorescent, metal
- Domestic: wood, fabric, wallpaper, carpet

### 6. Style Generics
- 1990s: warm, analog, boxy clothing, practical effects
- 1970s: earth tones, film grain, wide collars, wood paneling
- 1980s: neon, synth, shoulder pads, VHS
- Contemporary: digital, clean, LED, minimal
