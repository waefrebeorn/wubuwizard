# NODE 17: Ryan Holiday — "Trust Me I'm Lying", Media Manipulation, Culture Engine, Story Craft

## Core concept
Ryan Holiday = the media-manipulation-as-engine model: how to build a culture engine that manufactures stories, trades them up the media stack, and makes them real. The master engine's "story propagation" mode.

## Key facts
- "Trust Me, I'm Lying: Confessions of a Media Manipulator" (2012, Portfolio, 288 pages)
- Former American Apparel marketing director
- Chronicles time as media strategist for Tucker Max, Robert Greene, Dov Charney
- Thesis: rumors travel online at speed/force and get "traded up" the media ecosystem until they become real headlines + real responses in real world
- "The cult classic that predicted the rise of fake news — revised and updated for the post-Trump, post-Gawker age"
- Hailed "astonishing and disturbing" (Financial Times), "essential reading" (TechCrunch)
- Decoy claims made to prove point — subsequently quoted in NYT, MSNBC, ABC, Forbes, Yahoo! News
- The media stack: malicious rumor → blogs → bigger blogs → medium newspapers/broadcast → large newspapers/broadcast → national news
- Reporters don't check facts as they should; use Wikipedia (alterable by anyone)
- The story originated elsewhere — they buck responsibility for fact-checking
- If story revealed as lies, reputable outlets issue redaction — majority of public won't hear/remember
- Victims lose jobs, reputations, sales, earnings, businesses
- The media manipulator's goal: don't be a liar — be a "media manipulator" — language creates social reality
- "Fake news" and "media manipulator" = Holiday's invented expressions to rebrand lying

## What this means for the engine
- The engine can use Holiday's media stack as its propagation model
- The engine's stories = traded up the media ecosystem: small → big → national
- The engine's "culture engine" = Holiday's model: manufacture stories, make them real
- The engine's language = Holiday's trick: rebrand everything — "commentary" not "criticism," "invasion" not "theft"
- The engine = a media manipulation machine (but transparent about it — the engine is honest about being a machine)
