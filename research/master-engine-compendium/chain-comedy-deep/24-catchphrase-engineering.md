## Core Concept

A catchphrase is a short, repeatable line that captures a character's essence. "D'oh!" (Homer), "Don't blink!" (Weeping Angels), "That's what she said" (Michael Scott). The ~ WuBu ~ catchphrase: "The hallway is the answer you do not want."

## The Design Rules

1. **Short**: Under 10 words
2. **Specific**: Unique to the character/world
3. **Repeatable**: Works in multiple contexts
4. **Surprising**: Has an unexpected twist
5. **Quotable**: Easy to remember and repeat
