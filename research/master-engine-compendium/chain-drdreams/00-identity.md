## HOP 3: TECHNIQUE — "FOUND ROOM, GENERATED CROWD"

### Dr. Dreams' AI filmmaking method
1. **Find a real location**: Moonlight Rollerway in Los Angeles (real place)
2. **Composite in Grok**: Take a photo of the real place → comp skeletons/elements into it using Grok
3. **Feed as reference**: Use the composite as background reference for Seedance shot
4. **Generate the crowd**: "The room was found. Only the crowd was generated"
5. **Output**: AI footage that looks real because the background IS real

### The philosophy
- "Found" + "generated" = hybrid realism
- Real locations anchor the AI generation
