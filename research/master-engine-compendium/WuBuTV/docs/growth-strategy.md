## The Data (Buffer Study: 11.4M TikTok posts, 150K+ accounts)

### Posting Frequency Impact
| Frequency | Views Per Post Lift | Viral Ceiling (p90) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 post/week | Baseline | 3,722 views |
| **2-5 posts/week** | **+17%** | **6,983 views** |
| 6-10 posts/week | +29% | 10,092 views |
| 11+ posts/week | +34% | 14,401 views |

### Key Insight
"Posting more frequently doesn't raise the floor — it raises the CEILING."
- Median views stay ~500 regardless of frequency
- But the TOP 10% of posts perform MUCH better at higher cadences
- More posts = more chances for a breakout hit
- **The biggest jump: going from 1 → 2-5 posts/week**

### The Strategy
1. **Minimum viable**: 2-5 posts/week (17% lift, sustainable)
2. **Optimal**: 6-10 posts/week (29% lift, needs batch production)
3. **Maximum**: 11+ posts/week (34% lift, needs factory pipeline)

## Measuring Success

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Target | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Completion rate** | 70%+ | People watch to the end |
| **CTR (thumbnail)** | 5%+ | People click on the video |
| **Share rate** | 3%+ | People share with friends |
| **Follower conversion** | 2%+ | Viewers become followers |
| **Comments** | 1%+ | People engage |

### What to Track Per Video
1. Views (total reach)
2. Watch time (retention)
3. Completion rate (hook effectiveness)
4. Shares (virality signal)
5. Comments (engagement)
6. Followers gained (conversion)

### The Feedback Loop
```
Post → Measure → Identify what worked → Do more of that → Post again
```

If "What?" meme + Old UNC stares gets 2x the shares → make MORE of that.
If game show format gets low completion → cut it or fix the hook.
