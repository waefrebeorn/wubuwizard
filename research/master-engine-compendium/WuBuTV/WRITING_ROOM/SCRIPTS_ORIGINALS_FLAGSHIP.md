# ORIGINALS FLAGSHIP SCRIPTS — O-3 through O-9

Written 2026-09-26. @WuBuOriginals is the WuBuTV flagship (Structure B), so it
shoots first, and it had seeds but no scripts. These are the authoritative beats.
Seven episodes, each with a complete 7-block paste-ready prompt.

**Found footage, not broadcast.** The differences from every other channel:
- Diegetic timecode in the corner, always running. It is the only graphic.
- No title, no scoreboard, no lower-third, no ident sting.
- The camera does not go inside when a door opens. That is the discipline.
- UNC does not narrate the world. He attends to it.

## THE WRITING RULES FOR THIS CHANNEL
1. **One act.** He does a thing. That is the whole episode.
2. **The gag must read silent.** If a beat needs a line of dialogue, cut it.
3. **Never explain.** Not the numbering gaps, not the day counter, not Day 47.
4. **The chicken is never shown cooking.** That is the joke and it is older than
   the show.
5. **If he notices something strange, he responds to it as routine.** That is the
   entire character. Premise 61: he has never been surprised.
6. **No licensed faces. No real logos. No real brand packaging.**

## O-3 — THE UNIT HE DOESN'T OPEN
```
CHANNEL:     originals
FORMAT:      found footage
ENGINE:      normal -> absurd
JOKE TYPE:   observation (the camera's refusal is the joke)
RECURRING BIT: the door opens, the camera does not go in, he closes it
CHARACTER:   Old UNC
DURATION:    30s
```

### BEAT SHEET
```
[0:00-0:08] SETUP:   Wide corridor. He stops at a unit door. Padlock, stencilled
                   number. He has the key. He opens it.
[0:08-0:16] DEEPEN:  The door is open two feet. The camera stays where it is. He
                   looks in. Nothing visible happens. He is waiting for something
                   to happen, the way you wait for a kettle.
[0:16-0:24] PUNCHLINE: He closes the door. Locks it. Puts the key in his pocket.
                   Walks to the next unit. Same wait will happen there.
TAG:           The timecode keeps running through all of it. It never stops.
```
