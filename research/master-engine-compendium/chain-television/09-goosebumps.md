## Core Facts

- **R.L. Stine** (born 1943, Columbus, Ohio). Real name: Robert Lawrence Stine. Before Goosebumps, he wrote humor books for children (the "R.L. Stine" name was a pen name — he used his real initials). He also wrote adult horror under the name "Jovial Bob Stine." He was the editor of the humor book line at Scholastic before launching Goosebumps.

- **Goosebumps** (1992-1997, book series, 62 main series books + numerous spin-offs). The series was a massive commercial success — over 400 million copies sold. The books were aimed at children aged 8-12 and featured horror/survival stories with twist endings. The series name came from the physical reaction of "goosebumps" to fear.

- **The Ghostwriter Controversy:** There is a persistent claim that Stine used ghostwriters for Goosebumps. Multiple writers have come forward claiming they wrote books under Stine's name. Stine has denied this — he says he wrote all the Goosebumps books himself, with his wife (Jane Stine) as editor. The controversy is documented on Reddit (r/GooseBumps), in articles, and in interviews. The truth is unclear — Stine may have used ghostwriters for some spin-off series, but the main Goosebumps series was likely written by Stine himself. The ghostwriter claims are part of the "Stine as brand" narrative — the idea that "R.L. Stine" is a brand, not a person.

- **Stine's pseudonym:** Stine also wrote Goosebumps books under pseudonyms — "R.L. Stine" is the main name, but some books were credited to "R.L. Stine" with a different middle initial, or to "Jovial Bob Stine" (his humor pen name). The use of pseudonyms is part of the "Stine as brand" narrative.

- **Slappy the Dummy** is the recurring character of the Goosebumps universe. Slappy is a haunted dummy who appears in multiple books and became the mascot of the Goosebumps franchise. The 2015 Goosebumps film featured Slappy as the main antagonist (played by a puppet/animatronic). The 2023 Goosebumps series (Disney+) also featured Slappy. Slappy is the "recurring character" that ties the Goosebumps universe together — the same role the Old UNC plays in WuBuMovie.

- **The Goosebumps TV Series (1995-1998, Fox, 5 seasons, 73 episodes).** Produced by **Stanley R. Lloyd** and **Charles쇼ell** (sometimes credited as "Charles Showell Jr."). The series adapted many Goosebumps books into 22-minute episodes. The series starred **Nathaniel Parker** (as the adult host?) and featured a cast of child actors. The series was well-received and ran for 5 seasons.

- **The 2015 Goosebumps film** (Sony, directed by Rob Schmidt, starring Jack Black as R.L. Stine). A comedy adaptation that featured Slappy as the main antagonist. The film was a box office success ($113 million worldwide). Stine himself appeared as a character in the film. The film was more comedy than horror — it was a "Goosebumps for families" approach.

- **The 2023 Goosebumps series** (Disney+, 1 season). A more serious adaptation that focused on the Slappy character. The series was a prequel to the 2015 film, showing how Slappy became haunted. The series was produced by **Rob Lieber** and **Joe Sachs**.
