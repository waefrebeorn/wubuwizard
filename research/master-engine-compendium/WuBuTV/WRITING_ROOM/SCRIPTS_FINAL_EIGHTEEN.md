## WHY THE RELOCATION IS THE RIGHT CALL, STATED FOR THE RECORD

**No new channels. Adding a channel is your decision and neither agent may make
it.** But the ideas survive and the containers do not, and the mapping is
mechanical rather than tasteful:

- **Horror → Originals.** The found-footage grammar already *is* that channel. A
  polite hallway, a door that is neither open nor closed, a man who looks at a
  corner once — these were never "horror" so much as **the absence of a threat in
  an apparatus built for one.** The genre was the packaging; Originals is the
  engine.
- **Sitcom → Public Access.** The couch is already there, and the running gag is
  already geometry. "He ducks and nobody comments" *is* the height joke.
- **Arena → Public Access.** A sport with no rules is a community-access show with
  a sign. The absence of rules is the same gag as the absence of a scoreboard.

## THREE THINGS FLAGGED, NOT DISCOVERED

1. **O-17 will fight us harder than anything else in the hundred.** The entire
   premise is a corner with nothing in it, and "a plain empty corner... with no
   opening and no object in it" is exactly the absence-in-a-load-bearing-position
   class that produced the all-lit tubes, the unplugged board and the blank
   counter. I have written it as a positive — two bare walls meeting, bare floor,
   bare ceiling, one flat uniform surface — but **if Muse puts anything in that
   corner, the episode is dead.** Do not re-ask; use the stronger positive.

2. **O-18, O-19 and O-20 share a height gag and therefore a risk.** The joke is
   that UNC is much larger than the people around him and everyone behaves as
   though it is normal. **The model will want to fix the geometry** — put him on a
   box, crouch him, or draw the short women taller. All three episodes break if it
   does. I have written explicit bars on exactly that in all three, and the
   negative block names it. 6-tile the figures together, not each clip alone.

3. **O-20's fanfare is the only non-room-tone audio in the relocated set.** One
   brass hit at 0s, then room tone, then a laugh track at 18s. If the model gives
   a full music bed, the episode reads as a stadium rather than a community tape
   with a stadium stab in it — **the whole channel depends on the sound being
   wrong, not the picture.** Check with `audio_qc.py` against the room-tone
   control, and confirm the track is silent for the 2s-18s stretch.
