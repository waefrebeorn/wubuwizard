## THE PROPS CLASS, STATED ONCE AND PROPERLY

Nine times now the pre-send gate has caught the same failure, and it is worth
naming precisely because it is the most useful thing this workflow has taught me:

> **A prop the gag depends on being empty is the prop a generator will fill.**

Unlit tubes (S-14), an unplugged scoreboard (S-26), a blank counter (N-13), a blank
lanyard card and an empty corner (O-14, O-15), a blank lesson card (PT-10), a dark
wall sign (PT-14). Every one is a prop I wanted *absent*, and every one needed the
instruction rewritten as **a positive object that is on screen** — a flat matte
panel with a visible seam, a row of blank white cards square and level at one
reading, two painted walls meeting at a clean vertical seam.

**The fix is never a re-ask.** Re-asking the same prompt re-rolls the dice on the
same instruction. The fix is a *stronger positive*: give the model a surface it can
treat as the thing, rather than an absence it will helpfully resolve.

## FOUR FLAGS FOR THE GENERATION PASS

1. **PT-15's held piano note is the only sustained non-room-tone audio in the
   hundred.** One note, 30 seconds, no decay. If it decays, repeats, or acquires a
   chord, the episode is a piece of music rather than a joke about a piece of
   music. Check the audio track, not the picture.

2. **PT-18 puts a camera at the row's height, and "nobody mentions the human" is
   the whole gag** — so there must be no human, no hand, and no photographer in
   frame. The absence of a photographer is the subject. This is the props class
   again but applied to a *figure* rather than an object, and the negative block
   names it explicitly for that reason.

3. **PT-16 is the only clip in the network with two rendering styles in one
   frame** — eight stop-motion skeletons and one flat clipart icon. That is
   deliberate, and it is the one prompt where a model normalising the style would
   destroy the joke in a single frame. Tile the third row specifically.

4. **PT-13 and PT-14 both depend on a long static hold** — a skeleton absolutely
   still for 30 seconds, and a lit bulb at one brightness for 30 seconds. Idle
   motion is what a generator adds to fill a hold, so 6-tile both across the whole
   clip and compare every tile, not just the ends.
