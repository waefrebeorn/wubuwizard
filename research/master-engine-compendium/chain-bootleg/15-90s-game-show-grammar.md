## The 1990s Game Show Grammar

The 1990s game show has a specific visual grammar that's distinct from other eras.

### Era-Specific Elements

**Set Design:**
- Bright blue or red backdrop with geometric patterns
- Host podium (tall, imposing, with graphic panel)
- Contestant podiums (shorter, in a row)
- Tiered audience seating
- Scoreboard (digital, LED)
- Buzzer system (visible buttons)

**Lighting:**
- Bright, high-key, even
- Colorful (blue, red, yellow gels)
- No shadows
- Studio-standard intensity

**Camera:**
- Wide shot (show the whole set)
- Low angle for host (imposing)
- Crowd shot (audience reaction)
- Split-screen (for before/after)
- Push-in for prize reveal

**Costume:**
- Host: suit, bright tie, microphone headset
- Contestants: casual but neat (jeans, polo shirts, sweaters)
- Audience: mixed casual

**Audio:**
- Buzzer sounds
- Audience cheering
- Enthusiastic music
- Host's amplified voice
