## Core Concept

Culture jamming is the practice of subverting media messages by creating parody versions. Adbusters (founded 1989 by Kalle Lasn) is the movement's flagship: spoof ads, media interventions, Buy Nothing Day. The ~ WuBu ~ Television Universe IS a culture-jamming operation — it subverts television formats by filling them with absurd content.

## The Chain

### Adbusters → Culture Jamming
- Founded 1989 by Kalle Lasn and Bill Schmalz (Vancouver documentary filmmakers)
- Catalyst: 1988 "Forests Forever" TV ad by BC Council of Forest Industries — greenwashing
- Response: "Talking Rainforest" anti-ad — old-growth tree explains to sapling "a tree farm is not a forest"
- Couldn't buy airtime — CBC deemed it too controversial
- Key campaign: Media Carta — "Right to Communicate in constitutions of all free nations"
- Culture jamming heavily influenced by Situationist International + détournement
- "Trickle up" activism — culture-jammed Tiger Woods smile into Nike swoosh
- Buy Nothing Day campaign
- Occupy Wall Street — Adbusters sparked the movement

### Détournement → The Situationist Technique
Détournement (Situationist International, 1950s-1960s): take existing media and repurpose it with a new message. The original meaning is subverted by the new context.

~ WuBu ~ uses détournement: take existing television formats (sitcom, game show, news) and repurpose them with absurd content. The format is the original meaning. The ~ WuBu ~ content is the subversion.

## Application to WuBu Bumpers

Every ~ WuBu ~ bumper is a culture jam:
- Take a sitcom format → fill it with height opposition → the sitcom is jammed
- Take a game show format → fill it with existential questions → the game show is jammed
- Take a news format → fill it with absurd events → the news is jammed

## Pitfalls
- **Too political** — Culture jamming is inherently political. ~ WuBu ~ is absurd, not political. The jamming is aesthetic, not ideological.
- **Too obvious** — If the jamming is too heavy-handed, it's preachy. The ~ WuBu ~ approach: the format is straight, the content is absurd. The audience sees the jam.
- **Forgetting the format** — Détournement requires the original format to be recognizable. If you jam a format the audience doesn't know, it's not jamming — it's just weird.
