## Core Concept

Anti-comedy is the comedy of refusal: the setup creates an expectation, the punchline refuses to resolve it. The audience is left in uncertainty. That uncertainty IS the laugh.

## The WuBu Anti-Comedy

The Old UNC is an anti-comedy character. He walks down a hallway. The hallway breathes. He doesn't react. The audience waits for a reaction. It doesn't come. The bumper ends. The audience laughs because they were waiting for something that never came.
