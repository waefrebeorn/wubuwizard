# LORE v1 — the canon WuBu actually needs to write with

Written 2026-09-26. `UNIVERSE.md` names MEGA and the storage units in a single
line each, which is not enough to write an episode from. This is the expansion,
and it is **additive**: nothing here contradicts the canon, it fills it in.

**The rule for all lore:** it is described by people who use it daily and are
not awed by it. Nobody in this world is impressed by MEGA. The awe is the joke's
absence, and it only works if the lore itself stays flat and administrative.

---

## THE MEGA

**What it is:** a storage facility. Vast. Rows of units, some numbered, some not.
It is where the show keeps things, and where UNC lives.

**The one rule that generates the rest:**
- **A unit you do not have a key to is not locked. It is not yours.** UNC does
  not break locks. He is not a man who breaks locks. He has simply, over years,
  ended up in possession of keys to things he did not break into, and he has
  never once been curious about why he was given them.
- Units are addressed by number. There are gaps in the numbering. **Nobody has
  ever explained the gaps.** Do not explain the gaps. That is the whole
  recurring bit and it survives only as long as it is unexplained.

**Facts that are safe to state on camera:**
- Fluorescent lighting, always slightly one tube warmer than the others
- The corridor is longer than it looks from any single angle
- There is a day counter. It is always running. It is never explained what it
  counts. Premise 61: he has never been surprised.
- A chicken. Day 47 is referenced. **The chicken is a running reference and must
  never be shown cooking.** That is the entire joke and it is older than the show.

**What is NOT true, and must never be stated on camera:** anything about origin,
purpose, or why it exists. If a script needs it explained, the script is wrong.

## THE STORAGE UNITS

- Units are **corrugated steel**, numbered in stencilled block type, doors
  secured with a hasp and a padlock as a formality
- The padlock is always the same brand. Nobody chose that. It was just always
  there.
- **Interior contents are never shown in full.** A door opens, the camera does
  not go in, UNC looks at it, and he closes it. Repeat. This is the discipline
  that keeps the show from becoming haunted-house content.
- Laura's entrance in canon is "red curtain, music from a storage unit." Take
  that literally: a curtain in a unit, music playing. Do not rationalize it.

## THE VENDING MACHINE (O-series, verified in o1-mega-vending)

- A standard red glass-front machine. Snack and drink rows. It works.
- **It is the most ordinary object in the canon and it is the one that should
  unsettle an audience**, precisely because it is so normal.
- Rules of the machine:
  - It dispenses. It always dispenses. Nobody has ever got nothing.
  - The selection it makes is never wrong and never explained.
  - UNC treats it with the same flat courtesy he gives the door and the orb. **He does not thank machines.** He acknowledges them. Same thing.
- **The orb sits on top of it in o1 and that shot is canon.** If the orb is on
  the machine in any other episode, that is a callback, not a continuity error.

## LORE-HEAVY PHRASES (Colonel register, for News)
These are the lines that make the Colonel sound like he has sources when he has
never had a source:
- "Filed under weather." — his universal container for the inexplicable
- "The room was real. The crowd was generated." — the network's thesis, said
  flatly, once, never explained
- "You supplied the reaction." — his whole show in six words
- "The hollowness IS the function."
- "The ground still gives things back." — the penny, which is never resolved

**Do not let him say these twice in one episode.** Each is a closer. A closer
used twice is a sign-off, and he is not signing off, he is broadcasting.
