# THE 100 (RECONCILED) — the slate on the real nine-channel grid

Written 2026-09-29. Supersedes `THE_100.md`.

## Why this document exists

`THE_100.md` was built on eight channels, three of which are not in the
network: `WuBuHorror` (8), `WuBuSitcom` (6) and `WuBuArena` (5). It never
mentions Labs, Media School, Network or Archive, which between them had
30 complete prompts. `UNIVERSE.md` wins any conflict with a writing-room
doc, and `CHANNEL_BRIEFS.md` is titled *The 9 Channels (CANONICAL)*.

So the geometry was never in doubt. The stale document was, and it was
stale in a way that looked authoritative — which is why a hand-maintained
map of file-to-channel was believed instead of the files themselves.

**Everything below is counted from the files at generation time.** No
hand-kept list, because the last hand-kept list was wrong.

## The four rules every episode obeys

1. The world refuses to explain itself. Someone is completely calm about it.
2. It reads from a **silent frame**. If it needs dialogue to land, it is not ready.
3. **One load-bearing detail**, named, holding across a 6-tile.
4. Landscape 16:9, `~ WuBu ~` bug burned in, native audio only.
5. A negative in a load-bearing position is rewritten as a positive object.
