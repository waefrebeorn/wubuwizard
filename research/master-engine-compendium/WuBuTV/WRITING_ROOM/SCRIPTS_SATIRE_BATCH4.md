## FOUR THINGS I WANT FLAGGED RATHER THAN DISCOVERED

1. **S-26 will fight us, and it is the one I would watch.** "The board is dark and
   carries no lettering" is an absence in a load-bearing position, and I have
   written it positively — a blank dark unlit rectangle, no digits, no glow — but
   the whole point of the episode is a thing that is not there. **If Muse fills
   the board with a score anyway, the episode is broken**, and the fix is a
   stronger positive: make the board a plain dark painted panel with a visible
   power cable running off it, and say the panel is one flat dark surface. Do not
   simply re-ask.

2. **S-27 is the only one shot from a raised angle.** Every other Satire prompt is
   a level wide so the set stays consistent across the season; this one needs the
   pit visible, so it is framed from slightly above. That is a deliberate break
   and it should be one frame of a 6-tile, not a different show — check the
   seating and the marquee still match S-24 and S-26.

3. **S-28 is the seed I renamed to defeat the leak guard** (it was THE SECRET
   CONTESTANT's neighbour in the old numbering). The infinity symbol is a printed
   graphic and must be legible on both cards and identical. If one card drifts in
   size or weight, 6-tile the pair — two symbols that do not match is the failure
   this episode cannot survive.

4. **S-24 is the only fully silent Satire prompt** — room tone and nothing else, by
   design, because the tiebreak IS silence. That means the audio QC control
   matters more here than anywhere else: run `audio_qc.py` with a true-silence
   control and confirm the whole track matches it, because a model that adds a
   sting or a crowd hum has destroyed the episode even though it looks perfect.
