## Core Concept

Internet archaeology is the practice of excavating, preserving, and theorizing about digital cultural memory. The "lost media" community collects and curates missing, corrupted, or obscure media. The ~ WuBu ~ Television Universe is an internet archaeology project — it creates media that FEELS like lost media (found footage, VHS degradation, institutional broadcast) and the audience curates it.

## The Chain

### Lost Media Community
- Collective excavation of missing/corrupted/obscure media
- "Lost Media, and the Secrets of Digital Culture" (Nicole Lamerichs) — community is testament to audiences + their involvement with media
- Categories: confirmed (found), submitted (claimed but unverified), deadpooled (confirmed fake), researching (in-progress)

### Web Archaeology
- "Web Archaeology: An Introduction" (TMG) — "digging" + "reconstructing" as central methods
- 'memoryware' = software/hardware + crawlers/bots/curators/users through which web's history is documented AND constructed
- Internet archaeology = preserving + curating + theorizing digital cultural memory

### The WuBu Archaeology

The ~ WuBu ~ found footage format IS internet archaeology:
- The MEGA (4.95 hours of found footage) is the "excavated" media
- The Old UNC is the "archaeologist" — he walks through the footage, examining it

## Application to 10-Second Bumpers

Every ~ WuBu ~ bumper is a "lost media artifact":
- The VHS degradation says "this was found, not made"
- The institutional broadcast aesthetic says "this was transmitted, not created"
- The found footage format says "this was recorded, not scripted"
- The audience's role: "What is this? Where did it come from? What does it mean?"

## Pitfalls
- **Too polished** — If the bumper looks too clean, it doesn't feel like lost media. The VHS degradation, scan lines, and chroma bleed are essential.
- **Too explained** — Lost media is mysterious because it's NOT explained. The bumper should raise questions, not answer them.
- **Forgetting the audience** — The audience is the curator. They want to share, theorize, discuss. The bumper should be quotable.
