# 🎭 ~ WUBU ~ GENRE OVERLAP — The USP, Operationalized
> *Our unique selling proposition: the 5-genre collision.* Pair with [[UNIVERSE]]
> and [[TOOLKIT]].

## The Claim
No other show combines these five into one identity:
**found footage + anthology + game show + horror + comedy.**
We are not "found footage with comedy" — we are a *new genre*.

## The Collision Recipe (per bumper)
| Layer | What it contributes | WuBu example |
|---|---|---|
| **Found footage** | Diegetic, raw, REC-cam honesty | Shaky cam down a breathing hallway |
| **Anthology** | Self-contained, one bit per unit | Each bumper = one game, one joke |
| **Game show** | Format framing + scoreboard | Teal-orb contestant, ∞:0 board |
| **Horror** | Dread, liminal, uncanny-not-scary | Walls expand; the Old UNC stays calm |
| **Comedy** | The deadpan pivot that defuses it | He pats the wall: "It's okay." |

## How to Collide (the writer's move)
1. Pick the **primary** genre for the channel (NEWS=satire, ORIGINALS=found footage,
   SATIRE=commentary, LABS=technique, HORROR=ORIGINALS' dread).
2. Layer ONE secondary genre as the twist (game-show framing on a horror space;
   anthology self-containment on a found-footage clip).
3. The **comedy is the defuser** — the Old UNC's deadpan is what makes dread
   benign (see [[HUMOR_BLUEPRINT]] benign violation).
4. Never let two genres fight for the punchline. One game, one flip ([[TOOLKIT]] rule 2).
