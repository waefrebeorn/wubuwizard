- "Last and First Men" (1930) — future history of humanity across 2 billion years, 18 species, planetary + stellar engineering
- "Star Maker" (1937) — cosmic scale: the narrator's mind leaves his body, joins with other minds, becomes a cosmic consciousness that explores the universe and encounters the Star Maker itself
- Style: deep future + cosmic scale narrated in a cool, almost detached philosophical voice — the irony of distance
- "Would you know who you really are?" — the question Stapledon's work poses across time + scale
- "How would you know?" — the epistemological question at the heart of cosmic-scale narration
- The engine can use this mode: cosmic scale + deep future + ironic distance + the "would you know?" question
