## Complete Works List (beyond TOS)

### Pilots (1970s)
- **Genesis II** (1973, NBC, TV movie pilot). Starred Alex Cord as Dylan Hunt, a scientist awakened from suspended animation in a post-apocalyptic future. Directed by John Llewellyn Moxey. Roddenberry's attempt to recreate the "thousand worlds" premise of Star Trek with a team of heroes traveling to different societies. Mariette Hartley played a Tyranian defector. The worldbuilding was "filled in by Mariette Hartley as a Tyranian defector" — the script had decent ideas but the background was exposition-heavy. Reworked into a second pilot called **Planet Earth**.
- **Planet Earth** (1974, NBC, TV movie pilot). Reworked version of Genesis II. Same Dylan Hunt premise, post-apocalyptic future.
- **The Questor Tapes** (1974, NBC, TV movie pilot). Android discovers humanity. Starred William Shatner (return to Trek universe without being Trek). Directed by Steven Hilliard Stern.
- **Spectre** (1977, TV movie). Supernatural detective series. Starred Nicol Williamson as John Spectre, a psychologist who sees ghosts. Directed by Robert Scheerer. Attempted to merge sci-fi with supernatural horror — the "Twilight Zone meets detectives" angle.

### 1970s TV Films
- **A.P.O. 923** (1973, TV movie). Also known as "The Quest for Love" in some markets. A group of people on a shuttle mission.
- **Pretty Maids All in a Row** (1971, theatrical film). Not sci-fi — a murder mystery drama. Roddenberry produced. Directed by Frank Perry.

### 1980s-1990s Series
- **Earth: Final Conflict** (1997-2001, syndication, 5 seasons, 78 episodes). Roddenberry's first non-Star Trek success. Post-apocalyptic Earth after an alien invasion. The aliens (the Taelons) appear benevolent but have a hidden agenda. Starred Kevin Conway as Jake Weaver, a resistance leader. Based on Roddenberry's original plot outline (written before his death in 1991). The series "fell to a number of setbacks" — production issues, script quality varied, but it ran for 5 seasons.
- **Andromeda** (2000-2005, syndication, 5 seasons, 110 episodes). Starred Kevin Sorbo as Captain Dylan Hunt (reviving the character from Genesis II/Planet Earth). Based on Roddenberry's original concept. Produced by Majel Barrett Roddenberry (Roddenberry's widow) after his death. Mixed reviews — "some critics find them flawed, with mixed opinions on Gene Roddenberry's other works."

### Unproduced Projects
- **Star Trek: Phase II** (1977, unproduced). Planned as a Star Trek TV movie that would lead into a new series. Cancelled when Star Wars success caused Paramount to greenlight Star Trek: The Motion Picture instead.
- **Star Trek: The God Thing** (unproduced). An early post-Trek concept.

### Created Television Series (Full List)
- The Lieutenant (1959-1961) — WWII drama, Roddenberry's first series
- Star Trek: The Original Series (1966-1969)
- Star Trek: The Animated Series (1973-1974)
- Star Trek: The Next Generation (1987-1994) — Roddenberry was creator/producer, though he had diminished creative control vs. TOS
- Earth: Final Conflict (1997-2001) — posthumous, based on his outline
- Andromeda (2000-2005) — posthumous, based on Genesis II concept

## 7-Step Linkage

1. The Lieutenant (1959) — first series → 2. TOS (1966) — breakout → 3. TOS films/TV movies post-1969 → 4. 1970s pilots: Genesis II → Planet Earth → The Questor Tapes → Spectre → 5. Ill-fated 1970s attempts (none picked up as series) → 6. 1980s TNG (creator credit, diminished control) → 7. 1990s posthumous: Earth: Final Conflict (5 seasons, first non-Trek success) + Andromeda (5 seasons, Kevin Sorbo, revival of Dylan Hunt)
