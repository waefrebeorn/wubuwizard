## The WuBu Bootleg Method

The ~ WuBu ~ Television Universe applies the bootleg research through a specific workflow:

### Step 1: Choose the Format Grammar
Each of the 9 channels has a format grammar. Start by selecting the channel and its grammar:
- NEWS → news desk grammar
- ORIGINALS → found footage grammar
- SATIRE → game show grammar
- LABS → infomercial grammar
- ARENA → sports broadcast grammar
- SITCOM → multi-cam sitcom grammar
- HORROR → analog horror grammar
- TALK → talk show grammar
- COMMERCIAL → commercial grammar

### Step 2: Describe the Grammar (Not the Brand)
Write 3-5 sentences describing the visual grammar of the format. Use only generic descriptors:
- "Bright game show set, host podium, contestant podiums, audience cheering, studio lighting"
- NOT "The Price Is Right set with Plinko board"

### Step 3: Add the WuBu Character
Describe the Old UNC or other ~ WuBu ~ character using original descriptors:
- "A Black man with an afro and a black hoodie with yellow WuBu lettering, carrying a staff with a teal orb"
- NOT "Gandalf with a teal staff"

### Step 4: Add the Inversion
Describe the one element that's different — the joke:
- "Every character is short" (height opposition)
- "The hallway is breathing" (environmental absurdity)
- "The game show questions are existential" (content absurdity)

### Step 5: Specify the Format
Add the technical specifications:
- "10-second clip, 480p, whatever aspect ratio the scene demands"
- "No narrator, diegetic audio only"
- "~ WuBu ~ watermark"

### Step 6: Run the Three Tests
- Lawyer Test: Would a lawyer recognize a specific IP? (Should be NO)
- Swap Test: Could this work for any show in the format? (Should be YES)
- Generic Test: Is this a category or a specific instance? (Should be CATEGORY)

### Step 7: Generate and Review
Submit the prompt. Review the output. If it contains any trademarked elements, revise the grammar and resubmit.

## Pitfalls
- **Skipping the tests** — If you skip the three tests, you risk trademark infringement or AI moderation.
- **Inconsistent grammar** — If you establish sitcom grammar, don't switch to horror grammar mid-prompt unless the joke requires it.
- **Forgetting the inversion** — Without the inversion, you're just generating a generic sitcom or game show. The inversion is what makes it ~ WuBu ~.
