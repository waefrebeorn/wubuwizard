# PUPPET THEATER — FLAGSHIP SCRIPT SET (PT-3 … PT-8)

Six episodes, written 2026-09-29. PT-1 (THE RINK) and PT-2 (THE COACH) are
already shot and accepted, so this set is written to be shot *after* them without
a re-match: one set, one narrator register, one costume grammar.

## THE CHANNEL'S GRAMMAR, from the brief and the depot

- **Format:** stop-motion hybrid shorts with a narrator.
- **The Narrator** is deadpan and never jokes. That is the whole engine: the
  narration describes something impossible as though it were a nature
  documentary, and the comedy is entirely in the *sincerity* of the delivery.
- **The cast is a clipart asset library.** Skeletons, wizards, blobs, clip-art
  figures with hard black outlines. The joke is that they are *obviously*
  assets and are treated as fully real within the world.
- **Bootleg target:** Robot Chicken's stop-motion/clipart collision register,
  without any specific show's characters, sets, or assets.

**The load-bearing rule for this whole set:** the skeleton's anatomy must hold
across a 6-tile — ribcage, hand bones, and skull identical in every frame. PT-1
already proved this is the failure class that decodes perfectly and looks wrong.
**Anatomy is stated as a positive object in every prompt**, never as a negative.

## WHY THESE SIX

Puppet is the only channel with **zero render coverage** in the brief's own fill
status, and it now has two accepted shots. These six give it a run:

- **PT-3** the register works without anything ever being handed over
- **PT-4** the manual describes only itself
- **PT-5** the queue has always had a fifth place
- **PT-6** two identical clerks, and neither is surprised
- **PT-7** the sign is simply correct about the room
- **PT-8** the tape is four hours long and this is all of it

**One set grammar, two casts** — skeletons (PT-3, 4, 6, 8) and wizards (PT-5,
7) — so PT-3, PT-4, PT-6 and PT-8 can be cut back to back on the same table
with the same rig, and PT-5/PT-7 together on a second rig. Lowest drift cost,
same as the News six.

**Cross-scene check required** before hand-off: PT-6 has two skeletons and PT-3
has one, and the integrity refusal that killed the old S-15 was exactly a
duplication artefact. If PT-6's second skeleton triggers a refusal, drop to
PT-3's single-clerk staging and carry the gag in the narration instead.
