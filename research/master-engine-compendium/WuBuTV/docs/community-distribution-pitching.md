## PART 1: COMMUNITY BUILDING

### The Insight
"Community doesn't grow from followers. It grows from trust." Community is not an audience — an audience watches, a community PARTICIPATES.

### Platform Comparison

| Platform | MAU | Best For | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Telegram** | 1B | Broadcast + private | 200K group size, no algorithm, bots | No discovery |
| **Discord** | 259M | Real-time community | Voice, video, channels, live energy | Fees, structure limits |
| **Reddit** | 100M+ DAU | Public discovery | Organic reach at scale | You don't own the platform |
| **X** | 600M+ | Public conversation | Discovery, reposts, memes | Algorithm suppression |

### The WuBu Community Strategy
1. **Telegram**: Primary hub (we're already here). Broadcast channel + discussion group.
2. **Discord**: For real-time community (watch parties, lore discussions, fan art)
3. **Reddit**: r/WuBuMovie or similar for discovery
4. **X**: Public-facing content and meme distribution

### Community Building Principles
- **Owned vs Rented**: Telegram/Discord = rented (platform can ban you). Website/newsletter = owned.
- **Engagement loops**: Ask questions, run polls, create inside jokes, reward participation
- **Lore participation**: The ~ WuBu ~ world has mysteries (the Order, the Old UNC's identity, the teal orb). Let the community theorize.
- **User-generated content**: Encourage wizardposting-style fan content (like Smoking Wizard community)

## PART 2: MUSIC DISTRIBUTION

### The Options Compared

| Distributor | Cost | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DistroKid** | $22.99/yr (unlimited) | Fast, cheap, unlimited uploads | Sold to a VC firm (trust issues) |
| **TuneCore** | $29.99/yr per single, $49.99/yr per album | Strong analytics, social tools | More expensive |
| **CD Baby** | $9.95 per single, $29 per album | One-time fee (no annual) | No unlimited plan |
| **Too Lost** | $19.99/yr | Good for electronic/hip-hop | Newer, less proven |
| **Symphonic** | $24.99/yr | Good for independent artists | Smaller network |

### Recommendation for WuBu
**DistroKid** (despite the sale) or **TuneCore** for the cleveland_video/ album.
- DistroKid: $22.99/year for unlimited uploads. Fast to Spotify/Apple Music.
- TuneCore: Better analytics, social tools. Good if we want to track performance.

### Distribution Path
1. Master the 9 tracks (loudness normalization, -14 LUFS)
2. Upload to DistroKid/TuneCore
3. Get on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, TIDAL
4. Use YouTube Content ID to monetize when others use our music
5. Distribute music videos to VEVO, Apple Music (DistroKid video distribution $99/yr)

## PART 3: PITCHING (Path to A24-Style Deal)

### The Kane Parsons Path
Kane didn't pitch — his content went viral and studios came to him. But if we want to actively pitch:

### Pitch Deck Structure
1. **Logline**: One sentence that captures the concept
2. **The World**: Visuals, tone, aesthetic
3. **The Characters**: Who are they? Why do we care?
4. **The Content**: What exists so far (bumpers, MEGA, album)
5. **The Audience**: Who watches this? (horror fans, comedy fans, analog horror community)
6. **The Numbers**: Views, engagement, growth (when we have them)
7. **The Vision**: Where is this going? (Feature film, TV universe, franchise)
8. **The Ask**: What do we need? (Distribution deal, production budget, partnership)
