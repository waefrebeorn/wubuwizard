## Core Concept

Comedy timing is the art of knowing WHEN to deliver the punchline. Too early: the audience isn't ready. Too late: the moment has passed. The perfect timing is when the audience is leaning forward, waiting, and you deliver exactly what they didn't expect.

## The Timing Tools

- **The pause**: A silence before the punchline. The audience leans in.
- **The beat**: A moment of stillness. The audience processes.
- **The hold**: Holding a reaction. The audience waits for the break.
- **The rhythm**: The pattern of setup-punchline-setup-punchline. The audience learns the rhythm, then you break it.

## The WuBu Timing

In a 10-second bumper:
- **0:00–0:03 (Setup)**: Establish normal
- **0:03–0:07 (Misdirection)**: Deepen the wrong assumption
- **0:07 (The pause)**: A beat of stillness before the punchline
- **0:07–0:10 (Punchline)**: The flip

The Old UNC's deadpan IS timing. He holds his reaction. The audience waits. The hold is the comedy.
