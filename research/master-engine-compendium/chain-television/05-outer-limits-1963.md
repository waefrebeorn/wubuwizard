## Core Facts

- **The Outer Limits** (1963-1965, ABC, 2 seasons, 33 episodes). Created by **Leslie Stevens**. Unlike TZ, there was no single "host" on screen — instead, the show opened and closed with the "Control Voice" narration, voiced by **Vic Perrin** (not Joseph Cotten — common misattribution; Cotten hosted the 1995 revival).

- **Leslie Stevens** (1921-1998). Producer/writer/director. Created The Outer Limits as a response to The Twilight Zone's success, but with a more science-fiction/technology focus. Before TOL, Stevens created "The Lieutenant" (which Gene Roddenberry wrote for), and was involved in "Peter Gunn" and other series. He was a former Navy officer.

- **Joseph D. Salinger** (often cited as **LDSalzman** — actually the same person? No — LDSalzman was the story editor, Joseph D. Salinger was a different person). The story editor for The Outer Limits was **L.D. Salzman** (sometimes listed as "Joseph Salzman" — the naming is confusing). Salzman was responsible for shaping the stories and maintaining the show's tone.

- **The "Control Voice"** — the show's signature narration, spoken by Vic Perrin:
  > "There is nothing wrong with your television set. Do not attempt to adjust the picture. We are controlling transmission. If we wish to make it louder, we will bring up the volume. If we wish to make it softer, we will tune it to a whisper. We will control the vertical. We will control the horizontal. We can roll the screen, make it ripple. We can change the focus to a soft blur or sharpen it to crystal clarity. For the next hour, sit quietly and we will control all that you see and hear. You are about to experience the awe and mystery which reaches from the inner mind to... The Outer Limits."

- This is the ANTITHESIS of Rod Serling's warm, conversational host persona. The Control Voice is cold, mechanical, all-powerful — the show itself is a controlling entity.

- **Episodes**: 33 total across 2 seasons. Notable episodes: "The Galaxy Being" (S1E1, the pilot, about a man who receives a message from an alien civilization), "The Man with the Power" (S1E2), "The Sixth Finger" (S1E3, about a man who develops psychic powers), "The Human Factor" (S1E4), "The Borderland" (S1E5), "O.B.I.T." Allen**), "The Man Who Was Never Born" (S1E12, time travel), "The Invaders" (NOT the TZ episode — different story, S1E16, about tiny aliens), "The Blind Man" (S1E17), "Nightmare" (S1E18), "The Concerned" (S1E19), "The Premonition" (S1E20), "The Refuge" (S1E21), "The duplicate Man" (S1E22), "The Balance of Nature" (S1E23), "Lionel" (S1E24), "The Voices of Median" (S1E25), "The Conscience" (S1E26), "Last Supper" (S1E27), "The Human Side" (S1E28), "The Defining'" (S1E29), "The Matrix" (S1E30), "Cradle of Darkness" (S1E31, about a woman who travels back in time to kill baby Adolf Hitler — written by **Blossoms**?), "The Guests" (S1E32), "The Nest" (S1E33).
