Twelve episodes, written 2026-09-29. These are the two channels the fill status
still showed at ❌, and they are the two that make the other seven read as a
network rather than a pile of programmes.

# PART ONE: THE COLONEL'S CODEC (C-1 … C-6)

## THE CODEC IS THE ONLY FORMAT ON THIS CHANNEL

From `VOICES.md`, verbatim structure:

> Urgent open → AI news item → personal aside → non-sequitur → abrupt cut →
> [STATIC]

- **Voice:** The Colonel. Gravelly, deadpan, urgent-but-calm.
- **Openers:** "Snake, do you copy? Listen to me."
- **Closers:** "I'll leave you to your mission." → [STATIC]
- **Personal aside:** small real things. "I found a penny… proof the ground still
  gives things back."
- **Non-sequitur:** absurd connections that *almost* make sense.
- **Never** explain the bit. **Never** break the structure. **Never** name a
  competitor.

**A codec is a fixed skeleton with different content per episode.** So all six
prompts share one exact set and timing, and only the words inside move. That
makes them the cheapest batch in the network to render and the easiest to hold
consistent — and it is the point: it is one show that never ends.

**Cross-promotion is mandatory.** C-1 through C-6 each name a different channel
in the non-sequitur, so the network refers to itself. **No character ever says
the word "WuBuTV"** — that stays a canon bug. The channels are referred to by
what they do, never by name.

# PART TWO: THE ARCHIVE (A-1 … A-6)

## THE ARCHIVE IS THE OPPOSITE OF EVERY OTHER CHANNEL

- **Unedited found footage. Diegetic only. No voice at all.**
- The subject is the MEGA footage itself: storage units, corridors, the room.
- The recurring line is *"the room was found."* It is never said in the clip —
  it is how the channel is described, and the clip itself has no narration.
- This is the channel where the `~ WuBu ~` burn does the work that a voiceover
  would do everywhere else.

**One rule makes this channel work and it is a QC rule, not a prompt rule:** the
footage must be *boring* in the correct way. Nothing happens. The camera looks at
a thing. The gag is the length and the commitment, and it only reads if the shot
is genuinely unremarkable. A clip that "tries" to be eerie fails.

So the load-bearing detail in all six is **an ordinary, correctly-rendered, entirely
static piece of the world** — and the auditor is deliberately not the right check
here. The thing to 6-tile is *stability*, not drama.
