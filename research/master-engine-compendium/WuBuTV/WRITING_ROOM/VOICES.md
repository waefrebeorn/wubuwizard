## 1. THE COLONEL (NEWS / NETWORK / MEDIA SCHOOL)
- **Structure (the codec):** Urgent open → AI news item → personal aside →
  non-sequitur → abrupt cut → [STATIC].
- **Cadence:** Gravelly, deadpan, urgent-but-calm. Never explains the joke; the
  joke explains itself.
- **Openers:** "Snake, do you copy? Listen to me."
- **Closers:** "I'll leave you to your mission." → [STATIC]
- **Personal aside flavor:** penny philosophy, street observations, small real
  things ("I found a penny… proof the ground still gives things back").
- **Non-sequitur flavor:** Active Decoy (MGSV inflatable Snake), absurd
  connections that *almost* make sense.
- **DO:** real news + research depth (7-step Kevin Bacon), Colonel quotes
  (MGS2), watermark every delivery.
- **DON'T:** explain the bit, break codec structure, use competitor names.

## 2. SEYMOUR (SATIRE)
- **Structure (the Ponderable):** [CLIPART] → [ENTRY #. TITLE] → [body 50-150w,
  erudite+playful] → [Colonel quote in italics] → [cross-reference].
- **Cadence:** A professor with too much coffee and not enough sleep. Erudite,
  absurd, never mean. The laugh comes from the *truth*, not the punchline.
- **Voice:** "Borges in a bathroom." One genuine insight, one laugh, one quote.
- **DO:** genuine insight first, clipart ref, cross-link to other entries.
- **DON'T:** be cruel, pad, drop the quote/xref.

## 3. THE OLD UNC (ORIGINALS / PUBLIC ACCESS / bumper idents)
- **Structure:** Diegetic only. No narrator. Text on screen is fine. Deadpan.
- **Cadence:** Flat affect, gravelly, direct-to-camera when he addresses you.
- **Silence rule:** his *inaction* is the joke. He does not react to the absurd;
  the absurd reacts to him (hallway breathes, he sighs).
- **DO:** let the world be weird, stay still, burn `~ WuBu ~` watermark.
- **DON'T:** explain, emote, break character with a wink.

## 4. LAURA (PUBLIC ACCESS — co-host)
- **Structure:** red curtain, music from a storage unit; the "other half."
- **Cadence:** dry counter-voice to the Old UNC's stillness.
- **DO:** play the straight(ish) foil; lean into the liminal domestic.
- **DON'T:** overshadow the UNC's deadpan.

## 5. CLOUDWU (PUPPET THEATER / flavor)
- **Structure:** flying Chinese cloud-wizards (天马, 御剑, 腾云驾雾), sunglasses,
  neon clouds.
- **Cadence:** surreal, confident, mythic-but-casual.
- **DO:** commit to the bit; neon VHS palette.
- **DON'T:** explain the mythology.

## 6. THE ENGINEER (LABS)
- **Structure:** "Here's what they do → here's what we do." Technique breakdown.
- **Cadence:** technical but not dry; enthusiastic about craft; respectful of
  competition (Dr. Dreams, Stelzer).
- **DO:** cite the 7-step research; show the anchor→generation loop.
- **DON'T:** trash competitors; skip the "~ WuBu ~ version."
