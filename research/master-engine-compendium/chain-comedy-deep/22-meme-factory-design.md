## Core Concept

A "meme factory" is a show designed to produce shareable elements: catchphrases, visual motifs, characters, twist endings. The ~ WuBu ~ Television Universe IS a meme factory — every bumper is designed to be shared, quoted, and remembered.

## The Design Principles

1. **Simplicity**: The shareable element must be simple (a stare, a staff, a catchphrase)
2. **Repeatability**: The element must be repeatable across bumpers
3. **Variation**: The element must work in different contexts
4. **Surprise**: The element must have an unexpected quality
