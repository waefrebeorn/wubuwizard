## The WuBuMovie's Recurring Character

Every memetic television show has a "recurring character" — a character that the audience remembers, that recurs across episodes, that is the show's "brand." The Twilight Zone has Rod Serling (the narrator who recurs across episodes). The Outer Limits has the Control Voice (the voice that recurs across episodes). Tales from the Crypt has the Crypt Keeper (the host that recurs across episodes). Doctor Who has the Doctor (the character that recurs across regenerations). The Simpsons has Homer Simpson (the character that recurs across episodes).

The WuBuMovie's recurring character is the Old UNC.

## The Old UNC as Recurring Character

The Old UNC is:
1. **The visual:** A Black man with an afro, a ~WuBu~ hoody, a teal-orb staff. The Old UNC's visual is the WuBuMovie's "logo" — the audience remembers the visual.
2. **The voice:** A gravelly, deadpan voice. The Old UNC's voice is the WuBuMovie's "audio logo" — the audience remembers the voice.
3. **The personality:** A deadpan philosopher — flat, uncertain, dryly humorous. The Old UNC's personality is the WuBuMovie's "character logo" — the audience remembers the personality.
4. **The behavior:** Walking through storage units, staring at the camera, carrying the teal-orb staff, speaking to the audience. The Old UNC's behavior is the WuBuMovie's "action logo" — the audience remembers the behavior.
5. **The mystery:** The Old UNC's identity is unknown — is he human? Is he an android? Is he a ghost? Is he the Order's prisoner? Is he the Order's creator? The Old UNC's mystery is the WuBuMovie's "narrative logo" — the audience remembers the mystery.
