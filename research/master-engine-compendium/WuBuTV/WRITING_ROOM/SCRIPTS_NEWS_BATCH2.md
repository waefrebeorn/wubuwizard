## ONE RIG FOR THE WHOLE CHANNEL

**N-11 through N-16 are the same desk, the same backdrop, the same microphone, the
same black hoodie, the same locked-off medium shot and the same 3/10/18/26
timing.** That is the News anchor format, and it is the cheapest continuity
surface in the network: one set, one wardrobe, one framing, six episodes.

**They are also the same set as the Network codec (C-1…C-6) and Media School
(M-1…M-6)** — same anchor, same hoodie, same static cut. The only differences are
the strap line (REPORT vs CODEC vs DECOY) and the framing, so **please fire all
eighteen from one seed and 6-tile the anchor across all of them.** A News clip
whose anchor looks like a different person is a network continuity error, not a
channel one.

**The one that will fight us is N-13.** Its gag is a counter whose cards are
blank, and I have written it as a positive — one flat row of plain blank white
cards holding one identical state — but a mechanical counter is exactly the sort
of prop a model fills with digits. If digits appear, the episode is broken, and
the fix is the stronger positive, not a re-ask. Same class of risk as S-26's
unplugged board.
