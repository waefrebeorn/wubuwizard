# SATIRE FLAGSHIP SCRIPTS — S-11 through S-16

Written 2026-09-26. @WuBuSatire is second in the WuBuTV lineup (Structure B) and
shoots right after the Originals flagship. Six episodes, complete 7-block prompts.

**Broadcast, not found footage.** The differences from Originals:
- The `INF:0` marquee is canon and must read identically in every wide shot.
- The audience is teal spheres in black domino eye-masks. They are the audience.
  **They do not react.** Not once. Not applause, not gasps, not movement.
- UNC is deadpan and does not perform. The comedy is that the show treats its own
  impossibility as scheduling.
- The **mask is retired.** No cloth mask, no face on a stand, no mask of any
  kind appears in this or any future episode. (The audience domino masks are a
  different object and are unaffected.)

## WRITING RULES
1. **The show is not cancelled.** That is the recurring joke across all six.
2. **He never explains and never winks.** No line of dialogue references the
   other channels or the network.
3. **One act per episode.** The set changes state, or it does not.
4. **Reads silent.** A viewer with the sound off must still get it.

## PRODUCTION NOTES FOR ALL SIX
- **The audience never reacts.** In all six. Not applause, not a turn, not a
  glance. If a sphere moves, reject.
- **`INF:0` in every wide shot.** 6-tile the marquee on all six.
- **Two of these carry a text-drift risk:** S-13's rule card (generated text
  scrambles) and S-16's wall clock (clocks tick). Both are named in their
  LOAD-BEARING blocks and both have explicit re-fire instructions in the blocks
  themselves.
- **S-15's duplication is the highest-drift risk of the six.** If the two men
  differ in any detail, reject and re-fire — the identity lock is the episode.
- **No mask anywhere.** The audience domino masks stay; the retired cloth mask
  does not appear.
