## The WuBuMovie's Genre Overlap

The WuBuMovie is a genre overlap — a show that combines multiple genres into a single identity. The WuBuMovie's genres:
1. **Found footage** — the MEGA's aesthetic. Raw, unedited, diegetic, handheld. The found-footage genre is the WuBuMovie's "format."
2. **Anthology** — the WuBuMovie's structure. Each storage unit is a "story," each ceremony is a "story," each character encounter is a "story." The anthology genre is the WuBuMovie's "structure."
3. **Game show** — the WuBuMovie's framing. The Order is the "game," the Old UNC is the "host," the viewer is the "audience," the storage units are the "contestants." The game show genre is the WuBuMovie's "framing."
4. **Horror** — the WuBuMovie's tone. The storage units are uncanny, the Order is mysterious, the Old UNC is lonely, the MEGA is unsettling. The horror genre is the WuBuMovie's "tone."
5. **Comedy** — the WuBuMovie's humor. The Old UNC's deadpan delivery, the teal-orb staff's absurdity, the storage units' weirdness, the Order's rituals. The comedy genre is the WuBuMovie's "humor."

## The Genre Overlap as Identity

The WuBuMovie's genre overlap is its identity — the show is not "found footage with comedy elements" or "anthology with game show framing." The WuBuMovie is a NEW GENRE — a "found-footage anthology game show with horror and comedy elements." The genre overlap is the WuBuMovie's "unique selling proposition" — the thing that makes it different from other shows.
