# NODE 6: Robot Chicken / Seth Green — Satire Crossover, Stop-Motion, Pop Culture Invasion

## Core concept
Robot Chicken = the crossover/invasion model: taking pop culture properties and INVADING them with satire via a distinct format (stop-motion toys). The master engine can invade other shows/systems the same way.

- Started 2005; Green + Matthew Senreich created it

### Wikipedia: Robot Chicken
- Created by Seth Green + Matthew Senreich
- Format: "twelve-minute show consists of short unrelated sketches usually satirizing pop culture characters or celebrities"
- "Toys are employed as the players, animated via stop motion and supplemented by claymation"
- "The writers, most prominently Green, also provide many of the voices"
- "The show mocks popular culture, referencing toys, movies, television, games, popular fads, and more obscure references like anime cartoons and older television programs"
- "Much in the same vein as other comedy sketch shows as Saturday Night Live or Mad TV"
- Objects used: "socks, paper bags, and popsicle sticks" — anything can be the player
