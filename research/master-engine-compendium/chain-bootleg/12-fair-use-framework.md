## Fair Use in Visual Description

Fair use is a US copyright doctrine that allows limited use of copyrighted material without permission. For AI-generated visual content, fair use is the legal foundation of bootleg grammar.

## The Parody Protection

Parody is specifically protected under fair use. To qualify as parody:
- The work must comment on or criticize the original (or the genre)
- Only enough of the original may be used to "conjure up" the reference
- The result must be transformative

- They comment on television formats by exaggerating them
- They use only the visual grammar (not the content) to conjure the reference
- They add absurdity, deadpan humor, and the height opposition inversion
