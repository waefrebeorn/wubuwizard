## WHY THESE SIX

Each takes one beat from the 25-seed Satire block that nothing else in the season
has used, and each is **a single physical fact that does not change** — the same
rule the whole channel runs on:

- **S-17** two identical spheres, and the scoreboard does not move
- **S-18** he claps for sixteen seconds and not one sphere in the seating moves
- **S-19** the screen shows a recording of this same empty set
- **S-20** the mascot pulses exactly once and the show is simply over
- **S-21** the same four seconds, four times, and he never changes
- **S-22** an anniversary under paper streamers, with nothing whatsoever commemorated

**One set, one marquee, one wardrobe.** All six are the same stage with the same
black hoodie, so this is the cheapest batch in the Satire season and the easiest
to hold consistent. **Fire them all off one seed.**

**Three things I want flagged rather than discovered:**

1. **S-21 is the one that will fight us.** "Identical pose in four passes" is a
   negative-adjacent instruction and the generator will usually give you drift in
   the second and third passes. If it drifts, do not re-fire blind — that is a
   Muse consistency problem and the fix is the same seed with an explicit
   "frame-for-frame identical" note, not a fresh ask.

2. **S-20's single pulse** is a load-bearing *count*. Two pulses is a different
   episode. That is in the negative block but it is worth watching for, because a
   mascot is exactly the sort of thing a model animates enthusiastically.

3. **S-18's laugh-free applause** depends on the audio actually landing. The
   seated spheres must be silent, and the only applause is one pair of hands.
   That is measurable with `audio_qc.py` against a true-silence control — if the
   band energy in the seating moments matches the control, a crowd has crept in
   and the clip is wrong however it looks.
