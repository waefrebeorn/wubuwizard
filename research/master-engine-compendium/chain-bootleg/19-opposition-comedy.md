## The Opposition Principle

Comedy lives in contrast. The most reliable contrast: ONE element that doesn't belong. The audience sees the contrast immediately. The joke is the gap between the normal and the abnormal.

The one-tall-person principle: In a room full of short people, one tall person is funny. Not because tall people are funny, but because the CONTRAST is funny. The audience's brain immediately asks: "Why is that person tall? Is this a world where short is normal? Is the tall person the oddity?"
