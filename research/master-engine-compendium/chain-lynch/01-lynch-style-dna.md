# NODE 1: David Lynch — Style DNA, Dream Logic, Surreal Irony

## Core concept
David Lynch is the style anchor for the master engine. His work defines the visual/audio/emotional language the system operates in: dream logic, surreal irony, irrational art.

## What makes Lynchian style

### Dream logic
- Lynch nails "dream logic" — surreal/off landscapes, weird color-grading, ever-changing dreamscapes
- Mulholland Drive = a literal dream movie; opening shot places viewer in POV of someone crawling
- "David Lynch manages to nail dream logic so perfectly" (Reddit r/movies)
- His irrational art = the philosophical position: art as irrationality, not rationalization

### Influence on writing
- "David Lynch's surrealism influenced my writing" (Facebook group)
- Dream-state narrative as a technique — the system can use this as its writing mode

### Lynch's philosophy of ideas
- "Just realize the ideas that you fall in love with" — ideas as the engine (Instagram @universodavidlynch)
- "I think part of the reason ideas haven't come in is that the world of cinema is changing so drastically" (industrialscripts.com)
- "If it's real, and if you believe it, you can say almost anything" (Facebook group)
- "Fall in Love with the Idea" (Instagram reel @screenwritinginla)

### Visual language
- Surreal/off landscapes, weird color-grading, dreamscapes that ever-change
- Lynch tribute film, remembering David Lynch, impact on cinema (TikTok @consequence)

### Philosophy/symbolism
- Todd McGowan episode: "philosophy, symbolism and creative" — CINPIX DIRECTOR VOICES
- Lynch's work analyzed philosophically — symbolism as a system
