# NODE 10: Kayfabe / Wrestling as Media — Invented Reality, Persona Play, Breaking Kayfabe as Commentary

## Core concept
Kayfabe = the invented-reality model: a system that presents staged elements as real, maintains the illusion, and then BREAKS kayfabe as commentary. The master engine can use kayfabe as its operating mode: present the world as real, maintain the illusion, break it as commentary.

## Key facts

### Wikipedia: Kayfabe
- "Kayfabe is the portrayal of staged elements within professional wrestling (such as characters, rivalries, and storylines) as legitimate or real"
- "Although initially a wrestling term, Kayfabe has also been used to describe any suspension of disbelief or pretense of reality that deliberately blurs the lines between fact and fiction"
- "Kayfabe is essential to creating and maintaining the non-wrestling aspects of the industry, similar to other fictional entertainment; a wrestler breaking kayfabe is analogous to an actor breaking character"
- "Since wrestling is performed in front of a live audience, kayfabe can be compared to the fourth wall in acting, as little to no conventional fourth wall exists in wrestling to begin with"
- "Kayfabe was fiercely maintained for decades with the intent to deceive fans"
- "Kayfabe is even broken during shows on occasion, usually when paying tribute to deceased and retired wrestlers or when a serious injury genuinely occurs during a match"

### "Neokayfabe" era (Abraham Josephine Riesman)
- "In which storylines can become real life and vice versa, thus blurring the distinction between fact and fiction and giving the audience complicity in creating the spectacle"

### Outside professional wrestling
- Jesse Ventura as Governor of Minnesota: "politicians 'pretend to hate each other in public, then go out to dinner together'"
- I Love Bees / Halo 2 ARG: call-taker broke character during Hurricane Ivan — "kayfabe break in ARG"
- The Joe Schmo Show: "contestants were unaware they were surrounded by actors (with the actors intentionally breaking kayfabe at the conclusion and, sometimes, unintentionally during production)"
- VTubing as "digital kayfabe" (Cecilia D'Anastasio, Wired)
- "Wrestling personas and VTuber avatars are 'literally the same thing'" (Brennan Williams/Mace)
- Accounts parody current political/cultural events — fans participate in kayfabe, posting about main characters as if they themselves are civilians in the show

### Etymology
- "Carny-speak for 'be fake'" (Merriam-Webster theory, supported by Chael Sonnen)
- Or altered Pig Latin for the same
- "The term kayfabe was often used as a warning to other wrestlers that someone who was not 'in the know' was in the vicinity"
- Example: 1970s, Kamala's wife celebrated $5,000 prize — not realizing it was simply a storyline or kayfabe

### Playing With Research: "On Kayfabe, Reality, and Truth"
- "Kayfabe operates as microcosm for understanding the verbing, the processes, that construct, deconstruct, and reconstruct reality"
