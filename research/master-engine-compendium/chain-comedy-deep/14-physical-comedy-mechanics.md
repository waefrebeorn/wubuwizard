## Core Concept

Physical comedy is humor that comes from the body, not from words. Sight gags, slapstick, pratfalls, double takes — these are the oldest forms of comedy, and they're still the most reliable.

## The Mechanics

- **Sight gag**: A joke you see, not hear. The Old UNC staring at a breathing hallway.
- **Slapstick**: Physical mishap. The Old UNC walking into a door that wasn't there.
- **Double take**: The character sees something, looks away, then looks back in shock. The Old UNC sees the chicken, walks past, stops, turns back.
- **Pratfall**: A physical fall. The Old UNC trips over nothing.
- **Take**: A reaction to something. The Old UNC's flat stare IS his take.

## The WuBu Physical Comedy

The Old UNC's deadpan is the anti-physical-comedy. He doesn't react. That's the joke. The physical comedy comes from the environment (breathing hallways, moving walls, appearing doors) while the character stays still.
