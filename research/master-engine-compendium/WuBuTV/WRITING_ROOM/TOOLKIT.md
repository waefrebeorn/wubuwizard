## The 7 Joke Types
1. **Misdirection** — expect A, get B. Backbone of most bumpers.
2. **Analogy** — two unlike things reveal truth. Slower, smarter laugh.
3. **Exaggeration** — normal pushed impossible. Big physical laugh.
4. **Absurdity** — illogical but emotionally true.
5. **Wordplay** — language itself (text cards, lower-thirds).
6. **Observation** — something known but never articulated.
7. **Act-Out** — physically demonstrate; the demo is the joke.

## The 4 Scene Engines
1. **Misdirection Engine** — establish direction → deepen wrong assumption → flip.
   (Found footage, horror, talk.)
2. **Contrast Engine** — two incompatible things collide. (Game show, sitcom, commercial.)
3. **Absurd Engine** — normal → absurd event → serious reaction. (Infomercial, nature-doc.)
4. **Normal→Absurd** — show normal, then not-normal; the gap is the joke. (Universal.)

## The 7 Structure Rules (every bumper)
1. **But/Therefore** — every beat causal; no "and then."
2. **One game** — ONE funny thing; don't cram.
3. **Rule of three** — establish, reinforce, subvert.
4. **Punch word at end** — last word/frame is the button.
5. **No jokes in setup** — setup is straight; punchline does the work.
6. **Benign violation** — surprising but not threatening.
7. **Three threads max** — primary action + background gag + text layer.

## Format Map
| Format | Best engines | Best joke types | WuBu example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Found footage | Misdirection, Absurd | Misdirection, Absurdity, Act-Out | Hallway, impossible happens |
| Game show | Contrast, Absurd | Exaggeration, POV, Contrast | Existential game show |
| Sitcom | Contrast, Misdirection | Observation, Callback, Act-Out | Domestic, deadpan |
| Talk show | Misdirection, Contrast | Analogy, Wordplay, Observation | Interviews a teal orb |
| Horror | Misdirection, Absurd | Misdirection, Absurdity, POV | Scary space, unbothered |
| Commercial | Contrast, Absurd | Exaggeration, Wordplay, Analogy | WuBu product infomercial |
| News | Contrast, Misdirection | Observation, Exaggeration, POV | Reports absurd event |
| Infomercial | Absurd, Contrast | Exaggeration, Wordplay, Absurdity | Solves impossible problem |
