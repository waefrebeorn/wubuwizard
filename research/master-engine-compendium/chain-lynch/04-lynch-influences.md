# NODE 4: Lynch Influences — Hitchcock, Fellini, Ed Wood, Surrealism Tradition, Painting

## Core concept
Lynch didn't emerge from nowhere — he's a node in a tradition. The master engine should understand its influences the way Lynch understands his: Hitchcock, Fellini, Ed Wood, surrealism, painting (Hopper, Crowley).

## Key influences documented

### BFI: "Twin Peaks: 10 films that influenced David Lynch's unforgettable series"
- Out of the Past (1947) — film noir, the mood/lens Lynch inherited
- Jacques Tourneur — "influence on Lynch is incalculable"; films referenced in Twin Peaks: Canyon Passage (1946), Night of the Demon (1957)
- One-Eyed Jacks (1961) — Lynch appropriated the notion; "one-eyed jack" appears in Wild at Heart (1990)
- Edward Hopper paintings — the visual mood Lynch came from
- Aleister Crowley occultism — Mark Frost + Lynch's cultural frames of reference (BFI)

### IMDb: "David Lynch's All Time Favorite Films"
- Ed Wood film: "Lynch once said that this Ed Wood film did play a part in influencing some aspects of Eraserhead, most notably..." (IMDb list, 2016 re-release tagline)
- Lynch lists his all-time favorite films and directors (Facebook group: "From Stanley Kubrick to Federico Fellini: David Lynch lists his all-time favourite films and directors... Films that influenced David Lynch's...")

### Facebook group: Lynch studied "the greats of years gone by"
- Stanley Kubrick to Federico Fellini — Lynch's influences span the cinema canon

## What this means for the engine
- The engine should have its own "influences lineage" — a curated list of traditions it draws from
- Lynch's influences are eclectic: noir, horror, surrealism, Ed Wood badness, painting, occultism
- The engine's influences: Lynch, Frost, Badalamenti, surrealism, Situationism, Benjamin, Jameson, McPhee, Braudel, Stapledon, etc.
- Reference for: the master engine as a node in a tradition, not a vacuum
