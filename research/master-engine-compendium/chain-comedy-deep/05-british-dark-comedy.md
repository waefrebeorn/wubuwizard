## Core Concept

British dark comedy is a specific tradition: ordinary settings, dark topics, deadpan delivery, twist endings. It's the closest existing genre to the ~ WuBu ~ aesthetic.

## The Chain

### The League of Gentlemen (1999-2002)
- Created by Reece Shearsmith, Steve Pemberton, Mark Gatiss, Jeremy Dyson
- Set in a fictional town where everyone is grotesque
- The comedy comes from the characters treating their grotesqueness as normal
- Dark topics (incest, murder, deformity) played for laughs
- **WuBu connection**: The height opposition world is a League of Gentlemen construction — arbitrary rules treated as normal, dark topics played for laughs.

### Psychoville (2009-2011)
- Created by Reece Shearsmith and Steve Pemberton
- Five characters receive mysterious letters — the connection between them is revealed slowly
- Dark topics (blindness, dwarfism, serial killing) played for laughs
- **WuBu connection**: The Order's mysterious purpose is a Psychoville construction — the audience slowly discovers the rules.

### Inside No. 9 (2014-2024)
- Created by Reece Shearsmith and Steve Pemberton
- Anthology format: each episode is a new story, new setting, new characters
- Dark topics played for laughs, twist endings, "utter banality" before the dark reveal
- **WuBu connection**: Each ~ WuBu ~ bumper is an Inside No. 9 episode — a self-contained dark comedy story in 10 seconds.

## The British Dark Comedy Structure

1. **Establish the ordinary** — A normal setting, normal characters, normal situation
2. **Introduce the dark element** — Something is wrong, but the characters don't react
3. **Escalate the darkness** — The wrong thing gets worse, the characters stay calm
4. **The twist** — The audience realizes the truth before the characters do

## Pitfalls
- **Too dark** — British dark comedy is dark, but it's still COMEDY. The ~ WuBu ~ version needs the deadpan to lighten the darkness.
- **No twist** — The twist is the punchline. Without it, the bumper is just "dark."
- **Too slow** — British dark comedy takes its time. A 10-second bumper needs to compress the structure: ordinary (2s), dark (3s), escalate (3s), twist (2s).
