## The 1990s Sitcom Grammar

The 1990s multi-camera sitcom has a specific visual grammar that's distinct from other eras. For the ~ WuBu ~ height opposition bumpers, this is the target aesthetic.

### Era-Specific Elements

**Set Design:**
- Wood paneling (medium brown, not dark)
- Green or burgundy couch
- Coffee table with vase of flowers
- Framed family photos on the wall
- Kitchen visible through a doorway
- Stairs visible (leading to bedrooms)
- Warm wallpaper (subtle pattern)

**Lighting:**
- Warm, slightly overexposed
- Three-point lighting (key, fill, back)
- Soft shadows
- Amber/gold color temperature
- No harsh contrasts

**Camera:**
- Multi-camera setup (3-4 cameras)
- Medium shot (most coverage)
- Slightly high angle (looking down at characters)
- Snap zoom for reaction shots
- Wide shot for physical comedy

**Costume:**
- Boxy clothing (oversized jeans, button-down shirts)
- Earth tones and warm colors
- Sneakers, not boots
- Minimal accessories
- 1990s-specific: flannel shirts, high-waisted jeans, bomber jackets

**Audio:**
- Laugh track (warm, supportive, not canned)
- Studio audience applause
- Ambient studio noise
- No background music (unless it's a musical moment)
