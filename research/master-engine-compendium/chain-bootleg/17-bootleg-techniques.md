## The Physical Bootleg Aesthetic

Bootlegging has a physical history that informs the visual aesthetic:
- **Xerography**: Photocopy degradation, high contrast, lost detail, grain
- **Screen printing**: Limited color palette, misregistration, bold graphics
- **Risograph**: Spot colors, misalignment, texture, DIY aesthetic
- **Zine aesthetic**: Cut-and-paste, typewriter text, collage, handmade

These physical techniques create a visual language of "unofficial" that the ~ WuBu ~ aesthetic draws on. The VHS degradation in the found footage bumpers is the digital equivalent of xerography — it signals "this is not official, this is bootlegged, this is real."
