# IDENT SLATE v1 — WuBuTV

Written 2026-09-26 for **Structure B — A Foundation**. Ten channel idents plus a
network ident and a sign-on. This is the paperwork.

**What an ident is here:** the paperwork of a network that found its own
building. Not a logo sting, not a bumper. Each ident is the *found object* of
that channel photographed plainly, with the WuBuTV bug on it and the word
`WuBuTV` nowhere in the artwork. The bug is the only place the name appears.

## THE RULES, all four binding
1. **Locked-off camera. No push in, no pan, no cut, no animation.** 10 seconds,
   one continuous shot. An ident is a still that happens to have a soundtrack.
2. **The artwork never spells WuBuTV.** No wordmark, no title card, no lower-third
   naming the network. The bug carries the name. A generated wordmark WILL drift
   across frames — proven on the b1 idents and on Laura's chest print.
3. **The bug is exactly two lines, bottom-right, every frame, never animated:**
   `~ WuBu ~` over `WuBuTV`. Both legible at 405px-wide centre crop.
4. **Audio: room tone for the space, one event, silence.** No stinger, no swell,
   no tagline. An ident that rises is an ident that is selling something, and
   this network does not sell.

## The one-line register
A found thing, plainly lit, in a room nobody cleaned. **The ident is a document,
not an advertisement.**

## N0 — THE NETWORK IDENT (the one that matters)
**LOAD-BEARING:** a stencilled corrugated-steel storage unit door, unit number
**117**, dead centre, padlock, grey carpet, one fluorescent tube running a
different colour temperature.
**GAMBLE:** nothing happens at all. It is a door. The network's face is a
storage unit, and nobody says so.
**WHY:** WuBuTV is a foundation that found its own building. The flagship is
Originals, the room is real, and the ident is the door nobody opened.
**AUDIO:** fluorescent hum 0s–10s. At 3s one distant metallic settle from off
frame. At 7s a padlock shifting a millimetre without a latch. No music.

## I1 — @WuBuOriginals *(flagship)*
**LOAD-BEARING:** the vending machine, glass front, rows intact, the teal orb
resting on top of it, unmoving.
**GAMBLE:** the orb does not pulse. It has never been asked to and this is
administration, not a performance.
**AUDIO:** machine hum, the specific flat whine of a compressor that is on.

## I2 — @WuBuSatire
**LOAD-BEARING:** the empty contestant podium, white, centred, the dot-matrix
marquee above reading **INF:0**.
**GAMBLE:** the podium is empty and the show is not cancelled. It is simply not
on yet.
**AUDIO:** the room tone of a lit studio that nobody is standing in.

## I3 — @WuBuNews
**LOAD-BEARING:** the news desk with the glowing orange edge, no anchor, the two
studio monitors DARK, the `ON AIR` indicator lit with nobody behind it.
**GAMBLE:** the desk is empty and the light is still on. The broadcast is
happening; the broadcaster is late. Nobody panics about the gap.
**AUDIO:** studio silence with a faint transformer buzz. At 5s one chair creak,
off camera.

## I4 — @WuBuPuppet
**LOAD-BEARING:** the lit ICE RINK arch, one skeleton on the ice, mid-push,
stop-motion.
**GAMBLE:** the rink is open and empty except for that one skeleton working
alone at 3am. Nobody is timing it.
**AUDIO:** the compressor of an ice plant, and one blade-stroke on ice at 4s.

## I5 — @WuBuPublicAccess
**LOAD-BEARING:** a red curtain, half drawn, the low ceiling of a cable-access
studio, one chair.
**GAMBLE:** music is audible from behind the curtain and nobody on camera
notices it, because the curtain is where the music has always come from.
**AUDIO:** muffled music from behind the curtain, level and constant, no melody
you could quote.

## I6 — @WuBuSitcom
**LOAD-BEARING:** a standard interior doorframe, and a chalk mark on the lintel
showing a height nobody in the episode will ever reach.
**GAMBLE:** the mark is there for the whole show and no character looks up at it.
**AUDIO:** domestic room tone, a fridge, a clock that is not in sync with
anything.

## I7 — @WuBuArena
**LOAD-BEARING:** a stack of cardboard boxes on an arena floor, crowd barriers
empty behind, one rope light.
**GAMBLE:** the arena is set up and lit and there is no event. It is ready. It
has been ready.
**AUDIO:** a large empty room's echo on a single footstep.

## I8 — @WuBuLabs
**LOAD-BEARING:** a split frame — LEFT a generated crowd, RIGHT a real empty
room — the seam visible and unapologised.
**GAMBLE:** the two halves are presented as one continuous image. The seam is
the finding, and nobody is presenting it.
**AUDIO:** both halves, at once: crowd murmur left, room tone right. The
murmur is the same volume as the room. That is the joke, in audio.

## I9 — @WuBuMediaSchool
**LOAD-BEARING:** a hard-lit single object on a lecture desk under a
document camera — a blank white card, lying flat.
**GAMBLE:** the card is lit like evidence. It is being examined. There is
nothing on it and the examination continues.
**AUDIO:** a projector fan, close, and paper being set down once.

## I10 — @WuBuArchive
**LOAD-BEARING:** an unedited texture loop with a diegetic timecode overlay
running, nothing framed, no title, no bug beyond the standard two.
**GAMBLE:** the timecode runs and nothing is explained. The format is the whole
argument and the archive does not make it for you.
**AUDIO:** raw room tone, unprocessed, including the hum of its own recording.

## S1 — SIGN-ON / SIGN-OFF
**LOAD-BEARING:** the N0 door, same unit 117, same padlock, but the fluorescent
has gone cooler and the lock is now open and hanging.
**GAMBLE:** the door is open and it does not open further. Sign-off is the
building at rest, not an invitation.
**AUDIO:** the hum is quieter than the network ident. Nothing else.

## THE BUILD NOTE
**An ident is the easiest thing to get wrong and the most expensive.** Four b1
takes already exist and every one of them predates the WuBuTV name, so **all
four need re-firing with the two-line bug** — that is not polish, it is the
network's face.

The failure mode to avoid: asking for the word WuBuTV *in the artwork*. A
generated wordmark drifts between frames. The bug is rendered by us, not the
model. If the model draws the name, the name will be wrong in at least one
frame, and an ident is precisely the shot where every frame is load-bearing.
