## The Parody Defense

Parody is the strongest legal protection for bootleg visual description. To qualify as parody, your work must:
1. Comment on or criticize the original (or the genre)
2. Use only enough of the original to "conjure up" the reference
3. Be transformative (add new meaning)

## How to Evoke Without Infringing

The key insight: you're not copying the original. You're evoking the GENRE. You're describing the FORMAT. You're using the GRAMMAR.

- "A multi-camera sitcom with warm lighting and a laugh track" evokes the SITCOM GENRE, not any specific show.
- "A bright game show with a host podium and contestant podiums" evokes the GAME SHOW GENRE, not any specific show.
- "Found footage in a liminal hallway with VHS degradation" evokes the ANALOG HORROR GENRE, not any specific show.

The audience's brain fills in the specific reference. That's the magic — the audience does the work, and you never had to name a brand.
