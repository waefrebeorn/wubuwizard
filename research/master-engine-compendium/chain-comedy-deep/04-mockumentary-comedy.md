# Lilypad 4/25 — Mockumentary Comedy: The Documentary Format as Comedy Container
# Domain: Mockumentary | Chain: comedy-deep-25 | Depth: Leg 1
# 7-step Kevin Bacon: mockumentary → The Office → What We Do in the Shadows → format rules → WuBu application

## Core Concept

Mockumentary comedy uses the documentary format as a container for comedy. The characters know they're being filmed. The audience knows it's fake. The comedy comes from the gap between the documentary's seriousness and the characters' absurdity.

## The Chain

### The Office (UK, 2001-2003)
Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant created the modern mockumentary. The rules:
- Characters speak directly to camera (talking heads)
- The camera observes, doesn't intervene
- The comedy comes from awkwardness, not jokes
- The documentary format makes the absurdity feel real

### What We Do in the Shadows (2014-2024)
Taika Waititi and Jemaine Clement applied mockumentary to vampires. The rules:
- The vampires know they're being filmed (they talk to camera)
- The camera crew is in danger (the vampires are dangerous)
- The comedy comes from immortal beings dealing with mundane problems
- The documentary format makes the supernatural feel boring

### The WuBu Mockumentary

The ~ WuBu ~ found footage format IS a mockumentary, inverted:
- In The Office, the camera observes mundane people in a mundane setting
- In What We Do in the Shadows, the camera observes supernatural people in a mundane setting
- In ~ WuBu ~, the camera observes a deadpan philosopher in a supernatural setting

The Old UNC doesn't talk to camera (he's not a mockumentary character). But the camera observes him like a documentary crew. The format is: found footage as mockumentary, but the subject doesn't acknowledge the camera.

## The Mockumentary Rules (Applied)

1. **The camera observes** — It doesn't intervene, it doesn't cut away
2. **The subject is serious** — The Old UNC doesn't know he's funny
3. **The audience is complicit** — The audience sees what the character doesn't
4. **The format is the container** — Found footage IS the documentary format

## Session Context

This lilypad fills a gap: "mockumentary" was mentioned only 1 time in our existing research, but the ~ WuBu ~ format IS a mockumentary variant. Understanding the mockumentary rules helps us understand why the found footage format works.

## Pitfalls
- **Breaking the fourth wall** — The Old UNC should NOT talk to camera. That's The Office. ~ WuBu ~ is found footage, not mockumentary. The difference: found footage characters don't know they're being filmed.
- **Too much talking head** — Talking heads are for interviews. ~ WuBu ~ uses lower-third text instead.
- **Forgetting the danger** — In What We Do in the Shadows, the camera crew is in danger. In ~ WuBu ~, the danger is existential, not physical.
